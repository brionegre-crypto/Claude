# Be The Man: outreach email drafts

These drafts come from the Business Plan (Aug 2026), mainly §9 Marketing and Sales. The plan calls for two kinds of outbound email:

- **Channel 1, "The 200":** personal asks to people who already know you. Send 10 a week, one at a time.
- **Channel 4, "Borrowed audience":** pitches to podcasts and men's ministries. Send 5 a week, each one opening with an episode idea for that specific show.

Everything below follows the plan's rules:

- **Law 6:** Brittney is not in any story.
- **The credibility rule:** each email names something still unresolved.
- **No mass sends.**

Anything in `[brackets]` is yours to fill in.

**Free signup link used throughout:** `go.bethemansystem.com/framework` (Step 1)

---

## 1. Channel 1: the 200 who already know you

**Who gets it:** Men from City of Wisdom, men you've taught or counseled, and friends and family in your phone. Work through about 200 names over 20 weeks, 10 per Sunday income block. Keep a running list of who you asked and what they said.

**Rules from the plan:**

- Address each man by name and send it individually. Never BCC a group.
- Ask once. A no is a no, and you never bring it up again.
- Once someone says yes, something has to arrive every week.

### 1a. Email version

**Subject:** Can I send you something?

> [First name],
>
> I'm starting to put out something for men who are trying to get it right, especially men who had to figure out being a husband and a father without anybody showing them how.
>
> It isn't sermons, and it isn't motivation. Each week I share one system I'm actually running in my own house: the time, the trigger, and how I can tell whether it held. I'll also tell you what hasn't worked yet. I'm not writing this from the finish line.
>
> Can I send it to you? It's one email a week, on Thursday. If you'd rather not, that's completely fine, and nothing changes between us.
>
> If you're in, reply "yes" or start here: go.bethemansystem.com/framework. Step 1 is free and takes about twenty minutes.
>
> Brian

### 1b. Text / DM version (for men you usually text rather than email)

> [Name], I'm starting to put out something weekly for men trying to get it right as husbands and dads. It's real systems, not motivation, and I'm honest about what I haven't fixed yet. Can I send it to you? No pressure either way.

### 2a. Master pitch template

**Subject:** Episode idea for [Show]: "A goal says what. A system says when."

> Hi [Host first name],
>
> I heard your episode with [guest / topic], especially the part about [specific moment]. It's the reason I'm writing.
>
> **Episode idea: "Why your promises keep failing, and what to write down instead."**
>
> Most Christian men don't lack conviction. We meant it every time: on New Year's, on Sunday morning, in the car after the argument. What we're missing is a *when*. There is research behind this. Ninety-four studies (Gollwitzer & Sheeran) found that men who decide the time, the place, and the trigger in advance follow through about three times as often as men who rely on resolve.
>
> In the episode I'd walk your listeners through:
> 1. Turning one promise ("I'll be more present") into a system ("7:00, one child, phone in the other room").
> 2. Writing down what a *miss* looks like before the week starts, so one bad day doesn't cost a month.
> 3. The wife test: could she see the change from across the room without you announcing it?
>
> A bit about me: I'm a pastor of ten years in Huntsville, AL, married for eleven years, and a father of three. I grew up without a father and had to build all of this from nothing. I'm not past it. I run these systems in my own house right now, and I'll tell your audience what's still broken as plainly as what's working.
>
> I teach on video every week, so I'm comfortable on mic and on camera. Here's a recent video so you can hear me: [YouTube link].
>
> Would this fit [Show]? I'm happy to work around your recording schedule.
>
> Brian Greene
> Be The Man, *Break the cycle. Build the system. Be the man.*
> [YouTube channel link] · [Substack link]

### 2c. Follow-up (send once, 7–10 days later, as a reply to the same thread)

> Hi [Host first name], I'm bringing this back to the top of your inbox in case it got buried. If the timing is wrong, or it isn't a fit for [Show], no reply is needed. Thanks for the work you're doing for men.
>
> Brian

If there's no reply after that, log it as "no response" and move on.
