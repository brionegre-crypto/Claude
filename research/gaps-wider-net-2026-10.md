## Risks to deal with first

### 1. The name "Be The Man" is a registered trademark owned by someone else
- USPTO registration 5700693, "BE THE MAN", owned by ForCollegeForLife, Inc. Registered March 19, 2019, for "educational services, namely, conducting programs in the field of personal development" (class 41). Live as of the search.
- That's very close to what Be The Man sells: personal development programs, courses and groups. A registered owner can send a cease-and-desist or ask platforms to take content down, and the closer the services are, the stronger their claim.
- An earlier "BE THE MAN" mark for motivational speaking (serial 76619980) was cancelled in 2012.
- **Action:** book a one-hour consult with a trademark attorney before spending more on the brand (ads, print, the church kit). Ask about:
  - risk given that the registered mark covers the same class of services;
  - whether "Be The Man System" or another name is registrable;
  - whether the registration is still in use.
  Expect $150 to $500 for the consult.
- Source: https://trademarks.justia.com/877/52/be-the-87752904.html

### 2. Alabama §34-17A: the "certified marriage counselor" title (flag, as standing instruction requires)
- §34-17A-4 makes it a Class B misdemeanor, unless licensed, to advertise marriage counseling services or to use a title such as "marriage counselor".
- The chapter exempts a "minister of religion … practicing within the scope of his or her expertise."
- What is not settled by the search: whether the exemption covers advertising to the general public online, charging a fee per session, and using the title on a commercial website. Couples in other states are under those states' laws.
- **Action:** a 15-minute call or email to the Alabama Board of Examiners in Marriage and Family Therapy (mft.alabama.gov), or the same trademark/business attorney, asking exactly that question. Keep the answer in writing.
- Sources: https://mft.alabama.gov/PDF/2018/Statute.pdf ; https://mft.alabama.gov/

### 3. No legal pages
- The Canva footer links YouTube, Substack and Facebook, but no privacy policy, terms of sale, refund policy or disclaimer. The checkout consent line mentions "the terms" and "the privacy policy" but links to neither.
- Stripe and most payment processors expect these. A refund policy matters most with a recurring subscription (the Club).
- The FTC's consumer review rule (penalties up to $53,088 per violation) applies once testimonials go up: use real ones only, with permission, and don't present unusual results as typical.
- **Action:** add four pages (Privacy, Terms of sale and refunds, Disclaimer, Contact) and link them in the Canva footer and the systeme.io checkout consent line. I can draft them for a lawyer to check.
- Sources: https://learnybox.com/blog/what-legal-pages-does-an-online-course-website-need/ ; https://www.bclplaw.com/print/v2/content/1552385/what-the-ftcs-final-rule-on-consumer-reviews-and-testimonials-means-for-your-business.pdf

### 4. Email deliverability (unverified)
- Gmail and Yahoo require SPF, DKIM and DMARC, one-click unsubscribe, and spam complaints under 0.3%. Gmail began rejecting non-compliant mail in November 2025.
- Every email goes out as brian@bethemansystem.com. Whether that domain is authenticated in systeme.io could not be checked from here.
- **Action:** systeme.io → Settings → Email → check the sender domain shows as verified. Then put bethemansystem.com into a free checker (MXToolbox, "DMARC lookup").
- Source: https://bird.com/docs/knowledge-base/deliverability/gmail-yahoo-requirements

## Channels nobody in this niche is using well

5. **A YouVersion Bible App reading plan.** YouVersion is the most-downloaded Scripture app, and content partners publish plans to it free. A 7-day "Be The Man" plan reaches men who would never find a website. Apply through a YouVersion content partner, or contact YouVersion directly. Sources: https://www.frontgatemedia.com/tag/youversion-reading-plans/ ; https://covchurch.org/youversion/
6. **Barbershops.** The Confess Project trains Black barbers as mental health advocates, and the NIH has funded barbershop health programmes. Black men trust their barber. A stack of 7 Tuesdays cards, with a QR code, in a few Huntsville barbershops is cheap and fits the audience. Sources: https://nimhd.nih.gov/news-events/features/community-health/barbershops.html ; https://www.wfyi.org/news/articles/how-barbers-are-cutting-mental-health-stigma-among-black-men
7. **Denominational conventions.** COGIC's AIM Convention (July 2026, Baltimore) and similar National Baptist and AME gatherings sell exhibitor space and run men's departments. A booth or a breakout session for the church kit, starting with the 2027 cycle. Source: https://www.bccenter.org/events/2026/church-of-god-in-christ-cogic-aim-convention-2026
8. **Being a guest on podcasts.** Faster than starting your own show. Pitch Black Christian marriage and fatherhood podcasts with one episode idea and one free offer (the 7 Tuesdays challenge). Sources: https://churchgrowthtips.beehiiv.com/p/get-on-christian-podcasts-as-a-guest ; https://podcast.feedspot.com/black_christian_podcasts/
9. **Amazon.** A print "7 Tuesdays" journal or the Husband's Devotional on Amazon KDP. Men's prayer journals are less crowded than women's, and Amazon is a search engine; every copy has the website in it. Source: https://www.zonguru.com/blog/best-selling-low-content-books-on-amazon

## Revenue nobody in this niche is using well

10. **Employer workshops.** Black men's employee resource groups book fatherhood and wellness workshops. Fees for this kind of talk range from about £1,200 (Fatherhood Institute seminars) to $10,000+ for known speakers. A 60-minute "systems for fathers" talk, faith-light, at $750 to $1,500 is a realistic start. Source: https://executivespeakers.com/Speaker/Doyin_Richards
11. **Fatherhood grants (longer term).** HHS Healthy Marriage and Responsible Fatherhood grants fund faith-based and community organizations. The current cohort runs 2025 to 2030, so the realistic path now is partnering with an existing Alabama grantee as a curriculum provider or facilitator, not applying directly. It requires a nonprofit or community-organization partner. Source: https://acf.gov/ofa/programs/healthy-marriage-responsible-fatherhood/about
