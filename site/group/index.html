Four seats · Founding price $147

You keep making the promise. Six Sundays to build what keeps it.

A live group for husbands and fathers who mean it every time and lose it by Wednesday. Four men. Sundays, 7:00 to 8:00 PM Central, on Google Meet. December 6 to January 24, with a break for the holidays.

Reserve my seat, $147 Talk to Brian first ›

Who it's for

  • You're a husband, a father, or both, and you want to lead your home better than you have been
  • You've made the same promise more than once: less phone, more patience, real prayer, home when you're home
  • You have ADHD (diagnosed or still wondering), and the usual advice hasn't held for you
  • You'll show up six Sundays in a row and do one small thing each week

What you get

  • Six live one-hour sessions with four men on Google Meet
  • The Complete Library, yours to keep: all 11 Be The Man tools, including the Weekly Scorecard
  • One check-in text each week between sessions

What happens each Sunday.

  • Dec 6: who are you building? You leave with one sentence that names him
  • Dec 13: goals with a when, and a clear line for what counts as a miss
  • Dec 20: what has stopped you so far, looked at honestly and without guilt
  • Jan 10: the laws of a system that holds. You set up your Weekly Scorecard
  • Jan 17: the four pillars. You adjust what the Scorecard showed
  • Jan 24: God at the center. You leave with a plan for the months ahead

No session December 27 or January 3. Enjoy the holidays; we pick up January 10.

It combines three things: a written plan with a time and a place (in two 2011 studies, children with ADHD who made if-then plans did better on tasks needing attention-shifting, working memory and waiting; those studies were with children, so we say built on, not proven), progress tracked on paper, and progress reported to other men.

The test: by January 24, could someone in your house tell something changed, without you telling them?
