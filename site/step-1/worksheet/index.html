Step 1 of 7 · The Be The Man System

What type of man do you want to be?

Not what you want to do. Who you want to be, on an ordinary Tuesday. Most men skip this and go straight to goals. Then the week gets hard, there's nothing underneath the goal, and it quietly goes.

  • 20 minutes in one sitting
  • A pen, if you print it
  • Somewhere quiet, after the house goes down

Type straight into this page. It saves on this device as you go. Or print it and write by hand.

1

Name him.

Write five qualities of the man you're becoming. Use your own words, not ones that sound good.

For example: Patient. Present. Faithful. Steady under pressure. Honest when it costs something.

2

Make each one ordinary.

Next to each quality, write what it looks like on a normal Tuesday. If you can't picture it happening on a specific day, it isn't specific enough yet.

For example: Patient becomes "I don't raise my voice when homework runs late." Present becomes "My phone is in the drawer from 6 to 8."

3

Apply the house test.

For each one, write how the people you live with would know, without you telling them. Not married? Use whoever lives with you, works next to you, or would notice first.

For example: "Dinner has conversation in it, not screens."

QualityOn a normal Tuesday, that looks like…They'd know because…
4

Write the sentence.

One sentence that says who he is. Build it from the five qualities above. Every part should be something you could check on a given day, and something someone else would see.

Aim for this"I am a man who is home when he is home, keeps his word to his wife, and lets his kids see him pray."
Not this"I want to be a better husband and father." True, and nobody could tell on Tuesday whether it held.

Check it before you call it done

Put it where you'll see it

This is the first system you'll build, and the smallest one. Pick one place and one time you'll read the sentence every day.

What comes next

Step 2 is your goals. Don't start it until this page is finished. Every goal you set from here gets checked against your sentence: does this move me toward that man, or is it something I feel I should want?
