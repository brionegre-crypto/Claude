Questions men ask first.

Who is Be The Man for?

Black Christian men with ADHD, diagnosed or still wondering, who struggle to turn good intentions into follow-through. If the usual productivity advice has not held for you, you are who it is built for.

What is the Be The Man System?

Four steps: write who God has called you to be, pick no more than three commitments, give each one a time, a place and a bad-day version, and score your week with other men. Scripture sets the standard. The structure makes it checkable.

What are the four steps?

1. The Calling: one sentence on who God has called you to be. 2. The Commitments: no more than three, written so you can check them. 3. The Floor: a time, a place, a trigger and a bad-day version for each. 4. The Ledger: a weekly scorecard reviewed with other men.

Do I need a diagnosis?

No. Men who are diagnosed and men who are still wondering are both welcome. In a 2023 CDC survey, 55.9% of US adults with ADHD got their diagnosis as adults, so many men find out late. If you want to find out whether you have ADHD, a licensed clinician can help. Brian cannot diagnose.

Is this therapy or medical treatment?

Be The Man is a faith-based coaching and consistency system, not medical treatment, diagnosis, or clinical therapy for ADHD. Brian Greene is a pastor and certified marriage counselor, not a physician or psychologist. It does not treat ADHD or any other condition and does not replace an assessment, medication or therapy from a licensed clinician.

Does Brian have ADHD?

Yes. He is still not a clinician, and nothing here is medical advice.

Is Step 1 really free?

Yes. Step 1 is a free worksheet with no card required. It arrives by email, and you can leave in one click.

Is it a Bible study or a devotional?

No. Scripture is the standard in every step, and each step ends in something you do and can check on your weekly scorecard.

What if I miss a day, or a week?

Then you have information, not a verdict. Each commitment gets a bad-day version, what counts as a miss is decided ahead of time, and the scorecard shows you what happened without judging it. Missing once is not failure.

Why a plan instead of more motivation?

Intentions rarely change a week, and an ADHD brain that loses track of time or drops the thread needs the plan outside its head. In two 2011 studies, children with ADHD who made if-then plans (“If X happens, then I will do Y”) did better on tasks that need attention-shifting, working memory and waiting for a bigger reward. Those studies were with children, and Be The Man itself has not been studied, so we say built on, not proven.

Is Be The Man connected to a church?

No. Be The Man is not run by a church or a denomination. It is for any Black Christian man who wants his faith to show up in how he keeps his word at home and at work.

What does the cohort cost, and what is the refund policy?

The founding rate is $197 for the six weeks, then $297. You get a full refund up to 24 hours before the first session. After that, you can move to the next round. You apply first, so we both know it fits.
