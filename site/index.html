Questions men ask first.

What is the Be The Man System?

A seven-step consistency system for Christian men. You write one sentence about the man you are becoming, set goals with dates, attach each commitment to a time and place, and track it on a weekly scorecard so you can see by Sunday night whether you kept your word.

Is Step 1 really free?

Yes. Step 1 is a free worksheet with no card required. It arrives by email in about two minutes, then you get one short email a week. You can leave in one click.

What are the seven steps?

1. What type of man do you want to be? 2. What are your goals? 3. What has stopped you so far? 4. The laws of a system that holds. 5. The four pillars. 6. Why you need a system. 7. God at the center.

Why a system instead of more motivation?

Intentions rarely change a week. In one study, 91% of people who wrote down when and where they would act followed through, against 38% who didn't, and the pattern held across 94 studies. The system turns a goal like "be more present" into "Thursday, 7:40 PM, phone in the drawer."

Is it a devotional?

No. The weekly emails teach the mechanism: when, where, what trigger, and how you would know it worked. Faith sits at the center, with scripture as the standard, but each step ends in something you do and can check.

Is Be The Man connected to a church?

No. Be The Man is not run by a church or a denomination. It is for any Christian man who wants his faith to show up in how he keeps his word at home and at work.

Who created it?

Brian Greene: a husband of eleven years, a father of three, a pastor of ten years and a certified marriage counselor, who grew up without a father in the house.

What does the Club cost?

The Be The Man Club is $19 a month, or $149 a year with everything unlocked on day one. Step 1 and the weekly email stay free.
