Questions men ask first.

Who is Be The Man for?

Black Christian men with ADHD, diagnosed or still wondering, who struggle to turn good intentions into follow-through. If the usual productivity advice hasn’t held for you, you’re who it is built for.

What is the Be The Man System?

A seven-step, faith-first system built around how your brain actually works. You write one sentence about the man you are becoming, set goals with dates, attach each commitment to a time and place, give each one a bad-day version, and track it on a weekly scorecard so you can see by Sunday night what held.

Do I need a diagnosis?

No. Men who are diagnosed and men who are still wondering are both welcome. In a 2023 CDC survey, 55.9% of US adults with ADHD got their diagnosis as adults, so many men find out late. If you want to find out whether you have ADHD, a licensed clinician can help. Brian can’t diagnose.

Is this therapy or medical treatment?

No. Be The Man is faith-based teaching and accountability. Brian Greene is a pastor and certified marriage counselor, not a physician or psychologist. It doesn’t treat ADHD or any other condition and doesn’t replace an assessment, medication or therapy from a licensed clinician.

Is Step 1 really free?

Yes. Step 1 is a free worksheet with no card required. It arrives by email in about two minutes, then you get one short email a week. You can leave in one click.

What are the seven steps?

1. What type of man do you want to be? 2. What are your goals? 3. What has stopped you so far? 4. The laws of a system that holds. 5. The four pillars. 6. Why you need a system. 7. God at the center.

Why a system instead of more motivation?

Intentions rarely change a week, and an ADHD brain that loses track of time or drops the thread needs the plan outside its head. In two 2011 studies, children with ADHD who made if-then plans (“If X happens, then I will do Y”) did better on tasks that need attention-shifting, working memory and waiting for a bigger reward. Those studies were with children, and Be The Man itself has not been studied, so we say built on, not proven. The system turns “be more present” into “Thursday, 7:40 PM, phone in the drawer.”

What if I miss a day, or a week?

Then you have information, not a verdict. Each commitment gets a bad-day version, what counts as a miss is decided ahead of time, and the weekly scorecard shows you what happened without judging it. Missing once is not failure.

Is it a devotional?

No. The weekly emails teach the mechanism: when, where, what trigger, and how you would know it worked. Faith sits at the center, with scripture as the standard, but each step ends in something you do and can check.

Is Be The Man connected to a church?

No. Be The Man is not run by a church or a denomination. It is for any Black Christian man who wants his faith to show up in how he keeps his word at home and at work.

Who created it?

Brian Greene: a husband of eleven years, a father of three, a pastor of ten years and a certified marriage counselor, who grew up without a father in the house.
