Leader guide

Keep the group going after week seven

Most men's groups end the same way: a good last night, a promise to keep in touch, and silence by February. The systems your men built only hold if someone keeps asking for the number. This lesson helps you choose what your group does next, and announce it on the night of session 7.

Option 1: a monthly report night

Meet once a month for 45 minutes. Each man reports his three numbers for the month, makes one adjustment, and prays with his partner. No new teaching. This is the lightest option and the one most groups can sustain all year.

Best for: groups where most men kept their numbers above half by session 7.

Option 2: run the seven sessions again

Start a new cycle with the same men, three months later. Each man writes a new sentence and keeps or replaces his goals. Men who've been through it once often go much deeper the second time, because they're no longer learning the method.

Best for: groups where several men struggled to get a system running, or where the group bonded and wants more time together.

Option 3: multiply

Ask two men from this group to lead the next one, with you sitting in for the first two sessions. Your licence covers every group your ministry runs, so there's no extra cost. This is how a men's ministry grows from one table to several.

Best for: groups with one or two men who clearly came alive during the seven weeks.

How to choose

  • Look at the group's numbers from session 7, not the mood of the last night
  • Ask the men privately which option they'd actually attend
  • Pick a date before session 7 so you can announce it that night
  • Whatever you choose, keep the partner pairs reporting every week

Your own check before you close the cycle

Before you plan the next thing, run the same test on your own life that you asked of your men: could someone in your house tell something changed in these seven weeks, without you telling them? A leader who keeps his own numbers is the strongest reason the next group will keep theirs.

If you or any of your men want to keep going individually, the Be The Man Club continues the same framework with new tools each month and a live monthly call. Couples in your group who want help together can ask about certified marriage counseling at bethemansystem.com.
