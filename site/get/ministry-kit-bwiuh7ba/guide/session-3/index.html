Leader guide

Session 3: name what has stopped you

What every man leaves with: one written sentence describing the exact moment his most important goal usually falls apart.

Hand out: the Step 3 worksheet, "What has stopped you so far?"

Read aloud: Romans 7:15 to 25.

This is the session where the room gets honest or doesn't. Your tone sets it. Go first in the report and first in the discussion, and name something real.

Report (15 minutes)

Each man says whether he told someone at home about his three goals: yes or no. Then one sentence on how they reacted. Keep it short. The stories can come out in the discussion.

The teaching

Paul describes doing the very thing he hates and not doing what he wants. Every man in the room knows that experience. The question tonight is not whether you've fallen short. It's where, exactly.

Most men didn't fail for lack of wanting it. Something stopped them, and it was usually the same thing every time. It's rarely a character flaw. It's a moment: a plan that depended on how you felt, one bad day that turned into a bad month, an evening with no decision in it, a habit you inherited and never agreed to.

Tonight each man takes his most important goal, thinks back to the last time it fell apart, and finishes this sentence: "It stopped when..."

Useful answers and useless ones

Useless: "I lack discipline." "I'm lazy." "I got distracted." You can't plan around any of those.

Useful: "It stopped when I got home after 7 and turned the TV on before I took my shoes off." "It stopped the first Sunday I missed, because I felt I'd already ruined it." "It stopped when my wife asked me about it and I got defensive." Each of those is a moment you could see coming.

When a man gives you a useless answer, ask him: "Where were you standing when it happened?" That usually gets you to the moment.

Discussion questions

  • What did Paul do with his frustration at the end of Romans 7?
  • Which habit did you inherit from your father that you never agreed to?
  • What time of day do your promises usually die?
  • What's the difference between shame about a miss and information from it?

Commit and pray

Each man reads his "It stopped when" sentence to his partner. The commitment for the week: notice the moment when it comes. Don't fix it yet. Just text your partner the day and time it happened.

Pray for each other specifically about that moment. Close on time.

Leader check

  • Every man wrote a moment, not a character trait
  • Nobody was pushed to share more than he chose to
  • You noted any man who mentioned something that needs a pastor or counselor, and followed up privately

If a man names abuse, addiction or an affair, this group is the wrong size of help. Talk with him privately this week and connect him to someone qualified.

Session 4 puts a time on the goal right where this moment sits, so the sentence needs to come back next week.
