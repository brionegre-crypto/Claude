Leader guide

Session 7: put God at the center of the plan

What every man leaves with: his whole plan (sentence, three goals, systems, failure lines and numbers) on one page, prayed over and corrected, plus a plan for the next twelve weeks.

Hand out: the Step 7 worksheet, "God at the center."

Read aloud: Psalm 127:1, and Matthew 6:33.

Plan for this session to feel different. Slow the teaching down and give the prayer time more room than usual. You can take five minutes from the discussion to do it.

Report (15 minutes)

Each man gives his number for all three systems. Then the question that matters more than any number: "Has anyone in your house noticed anything, without you telling them?" Let men answer honestly, including "not yet."

The teaching

"Unless the Lord builds the house, those who build it labor in vain." Six weeks of building, and tonight we check whose house it is.

A system with you at the center will serve you. That's the problem. Built around yourself, even a good system becomes a more efficient way to get what you want. So the center isn't you. The man in your session 1 sentence starts as the man God is making. Your three goals answer to Him before they answer to you. The source law already said it: the standard is scripture, not mood.

"Seek first the kingdom." Not first the system, not first the number. The system is how a man keeps seeking on the days he doesn't feel like it.

The final writing time

Each man lays out everything from the seven weeks on one page: the sentence, the three goals, the three systems with their failure lines, and his numbers from sessions 5, 6 and 7. Then he prays over it silently and asks one question of every line: is this who God is making me, or who I want to look like? He changes anything that answers the wrong way.

A hypothetical example: a man realises his goal of "a second income stream by spring" was about looking successful to his brothers-in-law. He doesn't throw it out. He moves it below the two goals that are about his household, and gives it one evening a week instead of four.

Commit and pray

Each man reads his finished page to his partner. Partners confirm their eight-week reporting agreement from session 6. Then pray as a whole group, with each man praying one sentence for the man on his left.

Before you close, tell the group what happens next. The final lesson in this guide gives you three options.

Leader check

  • Every man has one page with his whole plan on it
  • Every partner pair has a reporting agreement for the next eight weeks
  • The group knows the date and shape of what comes next
