Leader guide

Session 6: track it and report the number

What every man leaves with: systems for all three of his goals on one Scorecard, and an agreement with his partner to keep reporting numbers after the group ends.

Hand out: the Step 6 worksheet, "Why you need a system," and a fresh Scorecard.

Read aloud: Lamentations 3:40, and 2 Corinthians 13:5.

Report (15 minutes)

Numbers again, then one extra line from each man: "The adjustment I made last week did or didn't help." Compare this week's numbers with last week's out loud, as a group total. If the total went up, say so. If it didn't, say that too, and say it's information.

The teaching

"Let us search and try our ways." "Examine yourselves." Scripture assumes a man can look at his own life honestly and see what's there. The Scorecard is how you do that weekly instead of once a year at a conference.

You've been inspired before. A sermon, a conference, a video. It lasted until the middle of the week. Motivation is a feeling, and feelings have a shelf life. A system is what's still standing by Wednesday. It doesn't ask how you feel. It asks what time it is.

And tracking works best when someone else sees it. Across 138 experiments, people who monitored their progress reached more of their goals, and the effect was stronger when the progress was reported to someone else (Harkin and colleagues, 2016). That's why every man in this room has a partner.

During the writing time

Until now, most men have run a system for one goal. Tonight each man writes a day, time and failure line for his other two goals as well, all on one Scorecard. Three systems is the most a man should carry at once.

Walk the room and check each new system against session 4: does it have a clock time, and is the miss something you could see? Send back any that say "evenings" or "more often."

Discussion questions

  • What did you think when you first had to say your number out loud?
  • Who in your life already sees how you're doing, whether you tell them or not?
  • What will happen to your Scorecard the week after this group ends?

Commit and pray

Partners make one new agreement tonight: "After session 7, we keep texting our number every week for at least eight more weeks." Each man says it out loud. Then the usual commitment: Scorecard filled in nightly, number texted once a week.

Leader check

  • Every man has three systems on one Scorecard
  • Every pair has agreed to keep reporting after the group
  • You've decided what you'll offer the group after session 7 (see the final lesson)
