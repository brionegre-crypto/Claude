Month 6: the Men's Bible Study and the Discipleship Workbook

For five months you have built a system for yourself and your home. This month you take it outside your house. Step 7 put God at the center. The Bible Study and the Discipleship Workbook make that something you practice on a schedule and with other men, not only something you believe.

With this month, you have all eleven tools. From here on, the Club adds one members-only tool each month.

Choose your track

The Men's Bible Study is eight weeks built for a small group. If you can gather two or three other men, even on a video call, use this one. Pick a weekly time, send them the PDF and lead the first session yourself. You do not need to be an expert. Each session has the reading, the questions and the one thing each man commits to before next week.

The Discipleship Workbook is for the weeks you are on your own. It turns faith into written decisions with a time attached, using the same when-and-where planning you have used since Step 1. The tracker shows your eight weeks on one page.

Many men use both: the workbook on their own during the week and the study with other men on the weekend.

Your six-month review

At the end of this month, lay your Scorecards from month one beside this month's. Then answer three questions in writing:

  • Which line on your Scorecard has improved the most since month one
  • Which habit slipped, and what time or trigger would bring it back
  • Could someone in your house tell something changed, without you telling them

If you can answer the last one with a specific example, the system is working. Bring your answer to the next monthly call so other men can hear what six months looks like.
