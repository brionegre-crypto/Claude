The monthly live call and your accountability partner

The tools in this Club tell you what to do and when. These two things make sure someone notices whether you did it. Across 138 experiments, tracking progress helped people reach more of their goals, and it worked best when the progress was reported to someone else (Harkin, 2016). Your Weekly Scorecard is the tracking. The call and your partner are the someone else.

The monthly live call

When: the second Tuesday of every month, 7:00 to 8:00 PM Central. The next calls are November 10, December 8, January 12 and February 9.

Where: Google Meet, at meet.google.com/pzg-pjvw-yvu. It's the same link every month. Keep it inside the Club.

What happens: one hour with Brian and the other members. Each man who wants to says, in one sentence, what his Scorecard showed this month: the number of days he kept his system, and the one miss that keeps coming back. Then we work on the misses. Nobody is made to talk, and the call is not recorded.

Before each call

  • Have the last four Weekly Scorecards in front of you
  • Count your kept days as a number, for example 17 out of 28, not "pretty good"
  • Pick the one miss you want help with and write it in one line
  • Join two minutes early from a quiet room

Your accountability partner

Opt in once with the button below. On the first of each month you'll get an email introducing you to one other member. He is your partner for that month.

Every Sunday night, send him a photo of your Scorecard, and he sends you his. Your reply only needs one line: "Got it. What's the plan for the misses?" If he hasn't sent his by Monday morning, ask for it. That question is the whole job.

To stop, reply "stop pairs" to any Club email.

How to know it's working

At the end of each month, compare your kept days with the month before. If the number went up, or the same miss showed up less often, it's working. If it didn't, bring that number to the next call. That's exactly what the call is for.
