Month 4: the Father's Field Guide and Father and Son Cards

For three months you have built the system around your marriage. This month you extend it to your children. The research here is specific: across 16 long-term studies of more than 22,000 children, what was linked to how kids turned out was a father's direct engagement, not just being in the house. Being home and being engaged are different things, and the Scorecard is about to show you which one you are doing.

If you do not have children at home, use this month for a nephew, a younger man at church, or a son who has grown up. The cards work with any of them.

Start with an honest count

Before you open the Field Guide, add a line to this week's Scorecard: one-on-one time with each child, phone away, at least 15 minutes. Mark it every day. Most men are surprised by the number. That number is your starting point, not a verdict.

Then add two rhythms

Weekly, the Field Guide. Work one section a week in the same slot you used for last month's guide. Each section ends with one decision with a time attached. For example: "Every Tuesday at 6:30 PM, I take my son to the hardware store or the park, just us."

Twice a week, the cards. Use the Father and Son Cards in places where talking is easy and eye contact is optional: the car, a walk, washing dishes side by side. Ask the card's question, then follow up once. If he gives a one-word answer, that is still a conversation, and the next card will go better.

Check yourself on day 120

  • Your one-on-one count is higher than in week one
  • Four Field Guide sections done, each with a dated decision
  • At least six card conversations
  • One thing your child told you this month that you did not already know
