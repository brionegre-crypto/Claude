Month 4: rebuild the Floor for a hard season

Holidays, travel, a sick child, a new job. Hard seasons do not ask permission. A plan that only works in normal weeks is not finished.

This month

  • Name the next hard season on your calendar.
  • For each commitment, write a season floor: what still happens on the road, at a relative's house, or on four hours of sleep.
  • Tell your partner which weeks will run on the floor, before they come.

"His mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning" (Lamentations 3:22-23).
