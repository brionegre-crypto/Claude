Step 2: The Commitments

Your sentence becomes no more than three commitments you can check each week. Three is not a lack of ambition. It is how a plan survives a real week.

"Let what you say be simply 'Yes' or 'No'" (Matthew 5:37).

The rules

  • Three at most. If you add one, retire one.
  • Checkable. Each one is a yes or no with a number. "Be more present" is not checkable. "Phone in the drawer, 7:40 to 9 PM, four nights" is.
  • Answer to the sentence. Ask of each one: does this move me toward the man in my sentence, or is it something I feel I should want?
  • Define the miss first. Decide before Monday what counts as a miss, so Sunday is counting, not arguing.

Use it now

Write your three on this week's Ledger. If one has been missed three weeks running, it is not a character problem. It is too big, too vague or at the wrong time. Take it to Step 3.
