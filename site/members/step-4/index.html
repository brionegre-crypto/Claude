Step 4: the laws of a system that holds

Download the Step 4 worksheet (PDF)

Steps 1 to 3 gave you three things: who you want to be, what you are going after, and what has stopped you before. This step is where they meet. A system that holds is one that was built with your obstacles in mind, not one that assumes you will feel motivated every day.

Test each law against your Step 3 list

As you read each law in the worksheet, put your Step 3 obstacles next to it. For every obstacle, find the law that would have caught it. If an obstacle is not covered by any law you are applying, your system has a gap, and that gap is where the next attempt will break.

Take the man whose morning prayer kept dying on late nights. A system that holds for him does not just say "pray at 6 am". It also says when his evening ends, what counts as a miss, and what he does the morning after one.

Work it in writing

  • Write each law in your own words next to one of your goals
  • Match every Step 3 obstacle to the law that answers it
  • Mark any obstacle that no law covers yet
  • Rewrite one goal so it would pass every law

The Weekly Scorecard (Month 1 under Your monthly tools) is these laws put into practice, one week at a time. If you set it up during your first week, look at it again now: you will probably see why each column is there.

Before Step 5: write down the one law you have broken most often, and what you are changing.
