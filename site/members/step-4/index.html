Step 4: The Ledger

The Ledger is how you find out what is true. Not how you feel about the week. What happened.

"One who is faithful in a very little is also faithful in much" (Luke 16:10). "Iron sharpens iron, and one man sharpens another" (Proverbs 27:17).

How to score

  • Mark each day: ✓ kept, F floor (counts as kept), ✗ missed, – not scheduled.
  • Add it up as a number: 13 of 15, not "pretty good." Four out of seven is four out of seven.
  • Report it. Your partner, the call, or a brother you trust. The number goes to someone every week.
  • Take grace without shame. Write one honest, kind sentence to yourself, then one change for Monday.
  • Reset. No starting over next month, no making up lost days. Monday is a new week.

Use it now

Print the Ledger below, or copy it into a notebook. Put Sunday's review on your calendar with a time and a reminder.
