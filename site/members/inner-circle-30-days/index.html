Your first 30 days

The first month decides whether the Inner Circle changes anything. Men who get value from a room like this do four things early: they finish Step 1, they pick three goals, they send a Scorecard every week, and they take the hot seat once. This lesson walks you through each one, week by week, so by day 30 you have a system running and a number to show for it.

Week 1: name the man

Open the members area and do Step 1, "What type of man do you want to be?" Twenty minutes, one sitting, a pen. Bring your one sentence to your first Thursday class and say it out loud. If it could describe any man, the room will tell you, and you'll rewrite it that night.

Week 2: cut to three

Do Step 2. Write every goal you're carrying, then keep three that answer to your sentence. Send me the three by text before Thursday. At your first one-on-one we'll check each one against what your house actually needs.

Week 3: give them times

Do Steps 3 and 4. Name the moment each goal usually dies, then give each goal a day, a time and a written miss. Here's a hypothetical example: "Phone in the glove box at 7:15 PM, Monday to Thursday. A miss is any night it comes inside before 8:30." Put all three on your Scorecard and send the first photo that Sunday.

Week 4: take the hot seat

Ask for the hot seat. Bring your first three Scorecards and one miss you want help with, in one line. The group will push on it. That's the point. You leave with one change and the time you'll make it.

How to know your first month worked

  • You have one sentence, three goals and three systems, all written
  • You sent a Scorecard photo four Sundays in a row, misses included
  • You took the hot seat once and made the change
  • Your number in week 4 is higher than in week 1, or you know exactly why not

If three of those four are true, the room is working for you. If they aren't, say so at your one-on-one. We'll fix the system, not your effort.
