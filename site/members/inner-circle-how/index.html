How the Inner Circle works

The Club gives you the system and a partner. The Inner Circle gives you me, ten men who know your name, and someone checking your number every week. This lesson is everything you need to use it. Read it once now and keep it bookmarked.

The weekly class

Thursdays, 7:00 to 8:00 PM Central, on Google Meet: meet.google.com/tcg-zspn-cco. Same link every week. The first class of the founding circle is Thursday, November 5.

The hour runs the same way every week. For ten minutes, each man gives his number from last week: kept days out of planned days. For twenty minutes I teach one thing. For the last thirty, two men take the hot seat: the group works through their system with them, out loud, until each leaves with one change and a time to do it. Everyone gets the hot seat about once a month.

Camera on, phone off the table. What's said in the room stays in the room. If you have to miss, text me before 5 PM that day.

Your monthly one-on-one

Forty-five minutes with me, once a month, on Meet or by phone. We go through your Scorecard for the month, your three systems, and whatever is in the way: at home, at work, in your walk. I'll email you within your first week to set the first one, and we book each next one before we hang up.

Your weekly Scorecard review

Every Sunday night, take a photo of your Weekly Scorecard and send it to me. By Tuesday you'll get one line back: what to keep, or what to change. Not a speech. One line you can act on.

A Scorecard with misses on it is exactly what I want to see. A blank one is the only kind I'll call you about.

Your direct line

Within 24 hours of joining, I'll text you from my number. Save it. Text or voice-note me on weekdays and I reply within 24 hours. Use it for the moment you're about to break a commitment, not just after.

This is a brotherhood of men, not counseling or crisis care. If you or someone in your house is in danger, call 911. If you're in crisis, call or text 988.

Everything else that's included

  • A half-day intensive every quarter, on Meet or in person in Huntsville, to build your next 90-day plan
  • Every live group I run, at no extra cost
  • One certified marriage counseling session for you and your wife each quarter
  • Everything in the Club: all seven steps, every tool, and the monthly Club call

Check yourself before your first class

  • The Thursday class is on your calendar, every week
  • You've saved my number when it arrives
  • You've printed this week's Scorecard
  • You've read the next lesson, Your first 30 days
