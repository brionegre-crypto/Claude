Month 5: the Christian Marriage Workbook and the Prayer Strategy

In month three you worked on how you lead at home. This month you work on time together, and you widen your prayer from your wife to your whole household. The Marriage Workbook is built around one weekly block of time as a couple. In a study of about 1,600 couples, those who spent time together at least once a week were about 3.5 times as likely to describe their marriage as very happy. That is a link, not proof that the time caused it, but it is a link worth acting on.

The workbook: eight sessions, one evening a week

You already have a weekly card evening from month two. Turn it into a workbook evening: same night, same time, phones out of the room. Each session has a short reading, questions for both of you and one thing to try before the next session.

A practical tip: read the session yourself the day before so you are not leading it cold. If a session opens something hard, you do not have to finish it that night. Write down where you stopped and come back to it next week.

The Prayer Strategy: a list, a time and a record

Your morning prayer for your wife has run for three months. The Prayer Strategy gives you a written list of the people in your house, a specific request for each one and a column to record what happened. Keep the morning time you already have and add one name a day from the list. Once a week, at your Scorecard review, read back the record. Seeing answers written down is what keeps the habit honest.

Check yourself on day 150

  • Four workbook evenings done on your chosen night
  • Your prayer list written, with a request for every person in your house
  • At least one answer recorded in the prayer log
  • Your Scorecard still running every Sunday since month one
