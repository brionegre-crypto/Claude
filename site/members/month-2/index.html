Month 2: re-read your Calling

By now your sentence may have gone quiet. You stopped seeing it. That is normal, and it is the moment to bring it back.

"So teach us to number our days that we may get a heart of wisdom" (Psalm 90:12).

This month

  • Read your sentence out loud once, this week, to someone in your house or to your partner.
  • Ask: is anything in it no longer true, or missing? Rewrite it if so.
  • Move it somewhere new: a new lock screen, a new spot on the mirror. A new place gets seen again.
