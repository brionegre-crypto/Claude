Month 2: the Husband's Devotional and Couples Conversation Cards

Last month you built a weekly score. This month you point it at your marriage. Add one new line to your Scorecard: prayed for her by name. Then add one evening a week for the Couples Cards. By the end of the month you should have 30 mornings of prayer and four conversations on paper.

Mornings: pray for her before the day takes over

The devotional gives you one short reading and one specific prayer for your wife each morning. Keep it specific: "Lord, give her patience with the kids today" is a prayer. "Bless my wife" is a wish. In a randomized study, people who prayed specifically for their partner every day for four weeks were rated as more committed than people who only thought good thoughts about their partner. Four weeks is exactly this month.

Tie it to something you already do every morning, such as before you pick up your phone or while the coffee brews. On your Scorecard, mark each day you prayed.

One evening a week: the conversation cards

Pick a night and a time, for example Thursday at 8:30 PM after the kids are in bed. Phones go in another room. Draw two or three cards and take turns. The cards start light and get more personal, and that order is deliberate: closeness grows when questions go a little deeper each time, not when you start with the hardest one.

Your job is to listen more than you answer. If she says something you want to argue with, ask one more question instead.

Check yourself on day 60

  • At least 20 mornings marked on your Scorecards
  • Four card evenings that actually happened on the night you chose
  • One thing you learned about her that you did not know on day 30
  • If you missed an evening, a new night written down that you can keep
