Welcome to the club

You joined because you have meant it before. New Year's, a Sunday morning, the car after an argument. You meant it every time, and it did not hold.

This club exists to fix that. Not with more conviction, but with a system: a mechanism, not a mood. Everything in here is built to show up on an ordinary Tuesday, in a way the people in your house could see without you announcing it.

What your membership includes

Here is what comes with your membership. Steps 1 to 7 and Month 1 are open today. Pay yearly and everything is open today.

  • The 7-step framework. Every worksheet from Step 1 to Step 7, in order, one lesson per step. This is the spine of the club.
  • Your monthly tools. All 11 tools from the store, a new set every 30 days until you have them all by month six: the Weekly Scorecard and Forty Cards first, then the marriage, fatherhood and faith tools.
  • The monthly tool. A new Club-only tool each month that is not sold anywhere else. It shows up in your members area when it is released.
  • The Thursday email. One email a week: a time, a trigger, and a way to tell if it held.
  • The monthly call and your partner. One live hour on Google Meet on the second Tuesday of every month, and if you want one, an accountability partner to trade Scorecards with every Sunday.

How to use the members area

Do not try to read everything this week. Men who binge every tool feel productive for two days and then stall. The order that works:

  1. Read the next lesson, "Your first week in the club". It gives you exactly three things to do in the next seven days.
  2. Work the framework one step at a time. Each step builds on the one before it. Step 1 ends in a one-sentence statement of the man you want to be, and every step after it gets checked against that sentence.
  3. Use each month's tools when they open, one set at a time. A tool you picked for a reason gets used. A tool you downloaded because it was free sits in a folder.
  4. Come back every Sunday. Your weekly review is where the system either holds or gets fixed.

Downloading your files

Every lesson that comes with a file has a download link in it. PDFs are made to be printed and written on with a pen, not filled in on your phone. The trackers are Excel files: open them in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers and save your own copy.

The files are yours to keep. If you ever cancel, save them first; access to the members area ends when your membership does.

Before you move on: write down one line: the one area of your life where you most want this to hold (your marriage, your kids, your faith, or your discipline). It is the first thing you will check yourself against. If you want, reply to any Club email with it. It reaches Brian.
