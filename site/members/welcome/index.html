Welcome to the Club

You finished six weeks. You wrote who God has called you to be, cut your life down to three commitments, built a floor under each one, and reported your number to other men. That is not nothing. Most men never get that far.

The Club exists for one reason: so the plan you built keeps running after the cohort ends. The cohort built the system. The Club keeps the Ledger going and keeps you around brothers who will ask.

What your membership includes

  • The four steps, for reference. The Calling, The Commitments, The Floor and The Ledger, with the worksheet and the printable scorecard. Come back to them whenever a week falls apart.
  • A monthly reset. One short lesson every 30 days, six in all, each ending in one thing to do that week. Pay yearly and all six are open today.
  • The monthly live call. One hour on Google Meet, the second Tuesday of every month, 7 to 8 PM Central.
  • An accountability partner. Optional. A different member each month, and you trade scorecards on Sunday night.

How to use it

Do not try to read everything. Read Your weekly rhythm next, print the Ledger, and put Sunday's review on your calendar with a time. That is the whole job this week.

Be The Man is a faith-based coaching and consistency system, not medical treatment, diagnosis, or clinical therapy for ADHD.
