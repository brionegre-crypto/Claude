Step 6: why you need a system

Download the Step 6 worksheet (PDF)

By now you have a sentence, goals, an honest list of what stopped you, the laws, and the pillars. This step is the reason all of it has to live in a system rather than in your head. Men do not lack conviction. You meant it every time. What was missing was something that keeps working on the days conviction does not show up.

Mood versus mechanism

Picture two men with the same goal: a weekly date night. The first man plans to do it "when things calm down". The second has Friday at 7 written down, a sitter booked on the first of the month, and a rule that a cancelled Friday moves to Saturday, never to "sometime". Three months later, only one of them is still going on dates. Both of them meant it.

The difference is not character. It is that one of them built a mechanism, and the other relied on a mood.

Turn your work so far into one weekly system

  1. Take the goals you sorted under the four pillars in Step 5.
  2. For each, write a time, a miss and a test, the same way the Weekly Scorecard asks.
  3. Decide when your Sunday review happens and put it in your calendar as a repeating event.
  4. Decide who you will tell when you miss. That can be your wife, a friend, or your accountability partner.

Before Step 7: your system should fit on one page and run without you having to remember to be motivated. Send a photo of it to your accountability partner.
