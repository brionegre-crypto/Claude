Step 3: The Floor

The Floor is what keeps one bad Wednesday from ending the plan. It has two parts: a trigger, and a bad-day version.

"For the righteous falls seven times and rises again" (Proverbs 24:16).

Part 1: give it an address

Name what usually breaks each commitment: time slipping, evening fatigue, the phone, a crowded house, shame after a miss. Then lock the commitment to a day, a time, a place and a trigger. "Thursday, 7:40 PM, phone in the kitchen drawer." "After I park at home, before I go in, I pray for one minute." "This week" is not a time.

Part 2: build the floor

For each commitment, write the two-minute version you will still do on your worst day. Prayer becomes one verse and one sentence. Training becomes ten push-ups. Being present becomes five minutes of real attention when you walk in. A floor day counts as kept. That is the point.

Use it now

  • Every commitment on your Ledger has a written floor.
  • Your most-missed commitment gets a new trigger this week: earlier, or attached to something you already do.
  • When you feel the week slipping, drop to the floor on purpose. Zero is the only real miss.
