Step 3: what has stopped you so far?

Download the Step 3 worksheet (PDF)

You have had goals like these before. Some of them you have set more than once. This step is an honest look at why they did not hold, so the system you build next is designed around the real reasons, not the ones you tell yourself.

Look for the pattern, not the excuse

An excuse explains one bad week: "work was crazy", "the kids were sick". A pattern explains why the same thing happens every time. When you work the worksheet, go back to specific attempts and look for what they had in common.

For example, a man who has tried three times to start praying in the morning might notice that every attempt died the first week he stayed up past midnight. The obstacle was never prayer. It was that his evenings had no end time. That is something a system can fix.

Questions to push past the first answer

  • When exactly did the last attempt stop, and what was happening that day?
  • Did the goal ever have a set time, or only good intentions?
  • Who knew about it, and did anyone notice when it stopped?
  • What did you tell yourself the first time you missed?

Be specific and be fair. The point is not to feel guilty. Guilt is a mood, and moods do not build anything. The point is to have a clear list of what the system needs to survive.

Before Step 4: you should have at least one obstacle written down that you have never said out loud before. If you are willing, name it on the next monthly call. You will not be the only one.
