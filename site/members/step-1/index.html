Step 1: The Calling

Everything else answers to this page. When a commitment stops making sense, the problem is usually that you forgot why it is there.

What it is

One sentence on who God has called you to be, built from your roles: son of God, husband or future husband, father or future father, son, worker, brother. For each role you asked what God expects of you there, and what faithful looks like on a Tuesday.

"Write the vision; make it plain on tablets, so he may run who reads it" (Habakkuk 2:2).

Use it now

  • Read your sentence every day at the place and time you chose. If you no longer see it, move it to where your eyes already go.
  • Every 90 days, rewrite it. Seasons change. A new job, a new baby, a move: your roles shift, so the sentence should too.
  • If you lost it, do the worksheet again. It takes 20 minutes, or four 5-minute pieces.
