Step 5: the four pillars

Download the Step 5 worksheet (PDF)

A man can hold one area of his life together for a while by letting the others slide. He gets serious at work and disappears at home. He fixes his health and stops praying. The four pillars exist to stop that trade. Each one carries weight, and the structure leans when one is ignored.

Score yourself honestly on each pillar

As you work through the worksheet, place your Step 2 goals under the pillar each one belongs to. Most men find the same thing: their goals pile up under one or two pillars and leave another one empty.

An empty pillar is not a failure. It is information. It is often where the Step 3 obstacles were hiding, because nothing in your plan was protecting it.

Your action from this step

  • Sort every current goal under one of the four pillars
  • Circle the pillar with the fewest goals
  • Add one small, scheduled decision to that pillar for next week
  • Put that decision on your Weekly Scorecard

Your monthly tools are organized to help here. When a pillar is thin, use the tool that speaks to it: the marriage tools, the fatherhood tools, or the faith and discipleship tools.

Before Step 6: tell your accountability partner, or the monthly call, which pillar was empty and the one decision you added.
