Month 1: the Weekly Scorecard and Forty Cards

You finished Step 1 with a sentence that names the man you are building. This month you give that sentence two things it has never had: a daily action and a weekly score. By day 30 you should have four filled-in Scorecards and around twenty-five cards done.

Your next set of tools opens in 30 days. It builds on the Scorecard habit you start now, so the most important thing this month is not to skip a Sunday.

How the two tools work together

Forty Cards gives you one specific action a day. The Weekly Scorecard is where you count how many days you actually did it. The cards supply the action. The Scorecard shows you the truth about it.

Here is how one week can look. Your Step 1 sentence says you are a man who is present at home. On Monday you draw a card: "Ask your wife one question about her day and do not look at your phone until she has finished answering." You do it at 6:15 PM, right after you walk in. On Sunday night your Scorecard shows 5 out of 7 days. That is a number, not a feeling, and next week you try to beat it.

Your setup this week

  • Print the Scorecard, or open the spreadsheet version, and write your Step 1 sentence at the top
  • Pick a fixed time for your daily card, such as the first ten minutes after you get home
  • Choose one person who will see your Scorecard every Sunday
  • Put the deck where you will see it at that time, not in a drawer

Check yourself on day 30

Count your four Scorecards. If every one has a number on it, the habit is working, even when the numbers are low. If a week is blank, you have found your weak spot: write down what happened that Sunday and move your review to a time that still holds on a bad week. A blank page tells you more than a perfect one.
