Month 3: the Godly Husband Field Guide

Month three is where most men quit a new habit. A daily habit takes a median of about 66 days to become automatic, which means you are right at the point where it either sticks or slips. This month gives you something bigger to work on so the habits from months one and two have a reason to keep going.

The Field Guide runs for eight weeks, one section a week. You will finish it partway through month four, and that is fine: the point is the weekly rhythm, not the speed.

Set the rhythm before you open it

Choose one 30-minute slot a week and treat it like an appointment. Sunday evening works for many men because it sits next to the Scorecard review you already do. Read one section, answer its questions in writing, and pick the single action it asks for that week.

Here is how that can look. The section on leading at home asks you to name one recurring decision you have been leaving to your wife by default. You write down "what we do with Saturday mornings." Your action for the week is to bring her a plan for this Saturday by Thursday night, and ask what she would change. On your Scorecard that becomes one line: done or not done.

Use the tracker

The spreadsheet tracker lists each week's action so you can see the whole eight weeks at once. Fill in the action on Sunday and mark it on Saturday. Across 117 studies, structured marriage education measurably improved communication, and longer programs did better than one-off advice. Eight weeks of steady work is the long version.

Check yourself on day 90

  • Four sections done, each with its written answers
  • Four weekly actions marked done or not done, honestly
  • Your morning prayer and weekly card evening still on your Scorecard
  • One change your wife would notice without you pointing it out
