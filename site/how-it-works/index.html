How it works

Four steps. Each one ends in something you can check.

Scripture sets the standard. The structure handles the follow-through. This is built around how men actually change: from still wondering, to ready to plan, to trying and slipping, to keeping it. Not therapy or medical treatment.

Step 1 · Free · The Calling

Write who God has called you to be.

List your roles: son of God, husband or future husband, father or future father, son, worker, brother. For each one ask, "What does God expect of me here?" Use Scripture, not feelings. Then write one sentence: "I am a man who ___, so that ___."

You leave with: one identity sentence. Anchor: Habakkuk 2:2, "Write the vision; make it plain."

Step 2 · The Commitments

Turn it into three things you can check.

For each role, write what that sentence requires this week. Then cut the list to three, no more. Each one must be a yes or no with a number. Decide what counts as a miss before the week starts.

You leave with: no more than three written commitments across faith, home, health and work. Anchor: Matthew 5:37, "Let what you say be simply Yes or No."

Step 3 · The Floor

Give each one a time, a place and a bad-day version.

Name what will break each commitment: time slipping, evening fatigue, the phone, shame. Then lock it to a day, a time, a place and a trigger. "Thursday, 7:40 PM, phone in the kitchen drawer." Finally write the two-minute version you will still do on your worst day, so one bad Wednesday bends the plan instead of ending it.

You leave with: a trigger and a floor for every commitment. Anchor: Proverbs 24:16, "The righteous falls seven times and rises again."

Step 4 · The Ledger

Score your week with other men.

Every week you mark each commitment done or missed, report the number to the men in your group, take grace without shame, and reset for Monday. Four out of seven is four out of seven.

You leave with: a weekly scorecard (for example, 13 of 15 kept). Anchors: Luke 16:10, "One who is faithful in a very little is also faithful in much," and Proverbs 27:17, "Iron sharpens iron."

Questions.

Is Step 1 really free?

Yes. Step 1 is a free worksheet with no card required. It arrives by email and you can leave in one click.

Is this therapy or medical treatment for ADHD?

Be The Man is a faith-based coaching and consistency system, not medical treatment, diagnosis, or clinical therapy for ADHD. Brian Greene is a pastor and certified marriage counselor, not a physician or psychologist.

Is this a Bible study or a devotional?

No. Scripture is the standard in every step, and each step ends in something you do and can check on your weekly scorecard.

What if I miss a week?

A missed week is information, not failure. Every commitment has a bad-day version, and what counts as a miss is decided ahead of time. You report the number honestly and reset for Monday.

Do I need a diagnosis?

No. Diagnosed or still wondering, you are welcome. Brian cannot diagnose. A licensed clinician can.

What is the refund policy?

For the founding cohort: a full refund up to 24 hours before the first session. After that, you can move to the next round. Full terms are on the Refunds page.
