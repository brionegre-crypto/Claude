The 7-week challenge.

Seven weeks. One email a week. One action with a time attached, small enough to do that week. For Black Christian men, husbands and fathers who keep making the same promise.

The seven weeks

  • Oct 20: write one sentence about the man you're becoming
  • Oct 27: cut your goals down to three
  • Nov 3: name the one thing that keeps stopping you
  • Nov 10: give one goal a day and a time
  • Nov 17: write down what counts as a miss
  • Nov 24 and Dec 1: count it honestly, then put God at the center of it

Free. No card. Leave in one click. Joining after October 20? You'll start with that week's email, and the first step comes in your welcome email.

Don't do it alone.
Forward this page to one man you trust. Once a week, text each other one word: done, or missed. That's the whole accountability.
