The Low Seat Check · free · 5 minutes

How humble are you on a Tuesday?

Every man thinks he's humble. The people at his kitchen table know better. This check skips how humble you feel and asks what you actually do: when you're corrected, when you miss, when nobody's watching, when someone else gets the credit.

“When you are invited, go and sit in the lowest place.” Luke 14:10

How it works

Twenty statements. For each, choose how often it's true of you in the last month, not on your best week.

What you get

A score across five postures, the one that's thinnest, and one action for this week with a time on it.

Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

The Low Seat Check · second witness

A man asked you to be his witness.

He took a humility check and scored himself. Now he wants the truth from someone who sees him at home. Answer the same twenty statements about him. Be fair, and be honest: that's the favor he asked for.

“By the mouth of two or three witnesses every word shall be established.” 2 Corinthians 13:1
You'll see both scores side by side at the end.
