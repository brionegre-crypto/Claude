Who it's for

  • You're a husband, a father, or both, and you want to lead your home better than you have been
  • You've made the same promise more than once: less phone, more patience, real prayer, home when you're home
  • You'll show up six Sundays in a row and do one small thing each week

What you get

  • Six live one-hour sessions with four men on Google Meet
  • The Complete Library, yours to keep: all 11 Be The Man tools, including the Weekly Scorecard
  • One check-in text each week between sessions

What happens each Sunday.

  • Nov 15: who are you building? You leave with one sentence that names him
  • Nov 22: goals with a when, and a clear line for what counts as a miss
  • Nov 29: what has stopped you so far, looked at honestly and without guilt
  • Dec 6: the laws of a system that holds. You set up your Weekly Scorecard
  • Dec 13: the four pillars. You adjust what the Scorecard showed
  • Dec 20: God at the center. You leave with a plan for January

It combines three things research keeps finding: written plans with a time (in one study, 91% of planners followed through against 38% of non-planners), progress tracked on paper, and progress reported to other people.

The test: by December 20, could someone in your house tell something changed, without you telling them?
