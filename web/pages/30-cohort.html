Refunds: full refund up to 24 hours before the first session. After that, you can move to the next round. Full refund policy

You apply with a free 15-minute call first. Payment is arranged after the call, not before.

What each call is.

  • 10 minutes Scorecard roll call. Every man says his number.
  • 15 minutes One teaching, tied to a step.
  • 25 minutes Hot seat. One man's plan, troubleshot live.
  • 10 minutes Lock in your Monday.

Six weeks.

Week 1

The Calling. Write your identity sentence.

Week 2

The Commitments. Cut to three you can check.

Weeks 3 and 4

The Floor. Triggers, times, places and bad-day versions.

Week 5

The Ledger. Score, report, take grace, reset.

Week 6

The reset. Read your six scorecards and decide what stays. Graduates can continue in the Club.

Who this is not for

This is not therapy or medical treatment. It is not for a man who wants a quick fix or a guarantee. It is for a man who will write the plan, pick the time, and report the numbers honestly.

Be The Man is a faith-based coaching and consistency system, not medical treatment, diagnosis, or clinical therapy for ADHD.
