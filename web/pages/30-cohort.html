What you get.

Six live calls

Thursdays on Google Meet, with no more than {{cohort_seats}} men.

Your own portal

Work each step, tap your commitments each day, and do your Sunday review on your phone or computer.

Paper, if you prefer

Every worksheet and the weekly Ledger print. Report your number, or upload a photo.

Brian reads your work

Each step goes to Brian before the next one opens. Private messages and prayer requests go straight to him.

A partner

One other man who sees your weekly number and your Sunday answers. Nothing else.

Teaching you can rewatch

The teaching part of each call is recorded. The men's sharing is never recorded.

Who this is not for

This is not therapy or medical treatment. It is not for a man who wants a quick fix or a guarantee. It is for a man who will write the plan, pick the time, and report the numbers honestly.

Be The Man is a faith-based coaching and consistency system, not medical treatment, diagnosis, or clinical therapy for ADHD.
