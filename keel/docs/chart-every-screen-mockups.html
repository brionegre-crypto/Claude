Chart Table, every screen

Every screen follows the passage you chose (A): numbered waypoints for what to do, a red mark for what's next, events kept quiet, mono times, and ruled chart paper. Two new pieces make it feel like no other app: the Binnacle, a floating dark navigation bar with a red Log button in the middle (one tap to capture, hold to speak), and the Bearing strip, a slim day scrubber you drag to move through time on Tasks and Calendar.

How it works differently across the whole app

These are new ways to do things you can already do. All the old ways still work.

#CHANGEWHERE
1Binnacle navigation: a floating dark bar with a red Log button in the middle. Tabs you hide are still one swipe away. Calendar and Notes swap in for any tab you choose.Everywhere
2Log button: one line that turns into a task, event, note, meal or reminder. Hold it to speak.Everywhere
3One swipe rule: right = done or archive, left = later or snooze, hold = quick actions (Start focus, Break it down, Reply).Mail, Tasks, Home, Calendar
4Waypoint numbers on everything you have to do. Tap the number to tick it off, with a fix-mark animation and a haptic.Home, Tasks, Mail "Needs you", Calendar, Body
5Bearing strip: drag across the week to move between days.Tasks, Calendar
6Drag to open water: drag a task onto free time to schedule it.Calendar, Home
7Ask your library: ask a question and get an answer from your notes, with links to the pages.Notes
8Instant tabs: tabs stay loaded, and background work waits until you stop touching the screen.Everywhere
