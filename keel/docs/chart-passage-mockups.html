Today's passage, three ways

In all three, tasks are the main thing on screen. Events drop to a quiet line, the chart has no event labels, and the top bar (overwhelmed, brain dump, search, options, settings) is back. Pick one, or mix them (for example A's chart with B's Next card).

A · Waypoints

The chart stays but plots only your tasks, as numbered waypoints. The numbers match the list below, and the red one is next. Meetings become one quiet line.

How it will work differently (for your approval)
