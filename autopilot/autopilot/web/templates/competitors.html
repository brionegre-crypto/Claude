How to read this

Numbers come from Instagram's official Business Discovery API: followers, posts, likes and comments on the last 30 days of posts. Reach, views and saves are private to each account, so they're not compared. Engagement rate = (likes + comments) ÷ followers, per post (median), which is fair across account sizes. Accounts that hide likes are marked. Personal accounts can't be looked up.
