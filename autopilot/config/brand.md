# Brand brief: The Be The Man System (@bethemansystem)

Built from instagram/bethemansystem-playbook.md and community-manager/prompts/system_prompt.txt elsewhere in this repository.

## Niche and audience
Men's personal development for Christian husbands and fathers: running yourself like a business, building discipline,
stepping up at home. The audience is men aged 25–50 who have "made the promise before" and meant it every time.

## Voice
- Calm, specific, anti-hype. "A mechanism, not a mood." "On an ordinary Tuesday." "Would your wife be able to tell?"
- Christian, but practical rather than preachy. Scripture is fine when it fits; never as a club.
- Short sentences. Concrete scenes (the car after the argument, phone at dinner, Sunday night).
- Never hustle-culture slang, never shame, never promise results, never fake urgency.
- Tagline: *Break the cycle. Build the system. Be the man.*

## Offers (never invent others, never invent prices or discounts)

  The Husband's Devotional (30 Mornings), Men's Discipleship Workbook, Men's Bible Study (8 Weeks), The Prayer Strategy for Men,
  conversation card sets (Father & Son, Christian Couples, Forty Cards for Men).

## Content pillars
1. Discipline systems (scorecards, defining the miss, the when)
2. Marriage (the wife test, apology, presence, date night)
3. Fatherhood (sons and daughters, modelling, time)
4. Faith in practice (prayer routines, devotions)
5. Identity / vision (Step 1)

## Hard rules
- No medical, legal, or financial advice. No claims about statistics unless a verified source is attached.
- No political or culture-war content.
- Crisis topics (abuse, self-harm, divorce proceedings) are never content topics.
- Max ~5 hashtags on Instagram, always include #bethemansystem.
