# Be The Man price ladder (proposal, 2026-10-04)

**Status:** proposal for Brian's approval. Nothing below is live yet.

## The rule behind every price

Each price goes up with **how much support you get**:

- **Low ticket:** a tool you run on your own.
- **Medium ticket:** a set of tools, or the Club, which keeps you going past the first two months.
- **High ticket:** Brian live, in a group or with your spouse.

Each blurb gives three things: the **result the product is built for**, the **research behind its method**, and the **price per use**.

**One honest limit.** There is no data yet on results from *these* products. Nobody has studied them, and no customer results exist yet. So the data in every blurb is about the **method** the product uses. The copy says "built on" or "linked to," never "proven" or "will." This is the Evidence Law, and the FTC requires it too. After the first 6-week group, real results from your own men can sit next to the research (see `evidence-and-tiers.md`, "Building your own evidence").

## Why the Club changes, and how it stops losing money

**The problem now:** $12 a month unlocks every tool on day one. The whole store sells for $147. A man can join, download everything and cancel, and you made $12 on $147 of product.

**The fix has two parts: the price, and when things unlock.**

| | Monthly, $19 | Yearly, $149 |
|---|---|---|
| Day one | All 7 step worksheets + the Weekly Scorecard | **Everything:** all 11 tools + all 7 step worksheets |
| Each month | One more store tool unlocks, plus that month's Club-only tool | That month's Club-only tool |
| To own the whole library | Stay 10 months = **$190 paid** | Paid up front, **$149** |
| Live groups | $50 off every later group ($247 instead of $297) | $50 off every later group |

**Why it can't lose money:**
- **Monthly:** a member only has the full library once he has paid at least $190. That's more than its $147 store price.
- **Yearly:** $149 is paid before anything unlocks, which is more than the $147 store price.
- **Complete Library, $97:** this is the one-time "just the tools" option. The yearly Club costs $52 more and adds 12 tools nobody else can buy. That's the reason to choose the Club.

**Why it's still good value:** at $19 a month, the Club still costs **less than one workbook** each month, and members still get every tool eventually.

**Founding members at $12:** keep them at $12 with what they already have. Close the $12 offer to new members once the new prices go live.

**Club blurb:**
> **The Club: $19 a month, or $149 a year.** A new habit takes a median of 66 days to become automatic, and for some men up to 254 (Lally, 2010). A one-time download is usually finished long before that. The Club is built for month three. Join and you get all seven step worksheets and the Weekly Scorecard today, then a new tool every month, plus one tool built only for members. Tracking works best when someone else sees it: across 138 experiments, progress reported to others did better than progress kept private (Harkin, 2016). Pay yearly and the whole library unlocks on day one. That's $147 of tools, plus a year of new ones, for $149.

## Low ticket: blurbs with the data and the math

**Weekly Scorecard: $7**
> Built to help you keep the promises you write down. Across 138 experiments with almost 20,000 people, tracking progress helped people reach more of their goals, and it worked best on paper and shown to someone (Harkin, 2016). One page a week for a year comes to about 13¢ a week.

**Forty Cards for Men: $9**
> Built to turn one good intention into a daily habit. A daily action takes a median of 66 days to become automatic (Lally, 2010). Forty cards, one action each, gets you more than halfway, for 23¢ a card.

**Father and Son Conversation Cards: $9**
> Built for the conversations that don't happen on their own. Sixteen long-term studies of more than 22,000 children link a father's direct engagement, not just being in the house, to fewer behavior problems and stronger development (Sarkadi, 2008). That's 23¢ a conversation.

**Christian Couples Conversation Cards: $9**
> Built to bring you closer, twenty minutes at a time. In a lab study, questions that got a little deeper each time built more closeness than small talk did (Aron, 1997). In long-term research, couples who stayed married turned toward each other 86% of the time, against 33% for couples who split (Gottman). Less than a quarter a card.

**The Husband's Devotional, 30 Mornings: $9**
> Built to make praying for your wife a daily habit. In a randomized study, people who prayed for their partner every day for four weeks were less unfaithful during that time, and observers rated them as more committed, compared with people who only thought positive thoughts about their partner (Fincham, 2010). 30¢ a morning.

**The Prayer Strategy for Men: $9**
> Built to give your prayer a list, a time and a way to see answers. It uses the same daily, specific prayer for your family that the randomized study above linked to stronger commitment (Fincham, 2010). One price, used every day.

**The Godly Husband Field Guide: $19**
> Built to strengthen how you lead and love at home. Across 117 studies, structured marriage education measurably improved communication and relationship quality, and longer programs did better than one-off advice (Hawkins, 2008). Eight weeks for $2.38 a week.

**The Christian Marriage Workbook for Men: $19**
> Built around weekly time with your wife. Couples who spent time together at least once a week were about 3.5 times as likely to say their marriage was very happy (Wilcox & Dew, 2012; a link, not proof of cause). The workbook gives you eight sessions in a set order, for less than a dinner out.

**The Father's Field Guide: $19**
> Built to turn being present into decisions with a time attached. The research is clear that engagement, not just presence, is what's linked to how children turn out (Sarkadi, 2008: 16 studies, 22,000+ children). Eight weeks for $2.38 a week.

**The Men's Bible Study, Eight Weeks: $19**
> Built for men to work through scripture together. In a 14-year study of more than 66,000 married adults, those who practiced their faith with others more than once a week were about half as likely to divorce (Li, 2018; a link, not proof of cause). Eight weeks, eight men, $2.38 each per week.

**The Men's Discipleship Workbook: $19**
> Built to make faith something you do on a schedule, not just something you feel. It uses the same written when-and-where planning that, in one study, took follow-through from 38% to 91% (Milne, 2002). Eight weeks for $2.38 a week.

## High ticket

**6-week live group: $147 founding, $297 after**
> Built to make what you write down still hold in week six. Six Sundays, one hour each, four men. It combines the three things research keeps finding: written plans with a time (91% vs 38% follow-through in one study), progress tracked on paper, and progress reported to other people (138 experiments). It includes the Complete Library ($147 on its own). **Founding price: $147, for these four seats only. Later groups will be $297, or $247 for Club members.**

*FTC note:* this replaces the crossed-out "~~$297~~". You can't show a crossed-out price you've never actually charged. Saying what later groups will cost is true, and it creates the same urgency.

**Certified marriage counseling: $300 + $35 assessment**
> Built on a validated relationship assessment. Taken before the wedding, the assessment we use has picked out which couples later thrived and which divorced with about 80–85% accuracy. Couples who did premarital education were about 30% less likely to divorce (Stanley, 2006). You get three sessions built on your own results, plus the Couples Cards and the Christian Marriage Workbook to keep going at home. That's $100 a session.

*Internal note, not for the page:* packages built on this kind of assessment that we found online ranged from about $300 to $450, or about $75 a session, usually for 4–8 sessions. $300 for 3 sessions is within that range.
