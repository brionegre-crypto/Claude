# Proposal: a premium tier at about $300 a month (2026-10-05)

Status: approved 2026-10-06 (tier above the Club; Thursdays 7 to 8 PM CT; founding $197/mo for the first 8, then $297/mo or $2,970/yr). Built: see pilot/README.md, "Redesign, new offers and Inner Circle".

## What exists today

- **Free website visitor / email subscriber:** Step 1 worksheet, the 6-minute training, the Thursday email, the 7 Tuesdays challenge, YouTube.
- **Club, $19 a month or $149 a year:** the course portal (Steps 1 to 7 with worksheets), the store tools released over six months, the monthly call, an accountability partner, and the systeme.io community (community 76257). The community comes with the Club purchase (it's a resource on product 3443630), so free visitors can't get in.

## Recommendation: add a tier above the Club, don't turn the Club into $300

Moving $19 straight to $300 would lose nearly every current and future member; men don't make a 15x jump without having been helped first. The usual structure is a ladder: free → $19 Club (proves the method works) → premium tier (Brian's personal attention). The $19 Club becomes the feeder for the premium tier.

What makes $300 believable isn't more PDFs. It's access to Brian, a small private room, and someone checking your numbers every week.

## The tier: working name "The Inner Circle"

$297 a month, or $2,970 a year (two months free). Ten men per circle, by application.

| What he gets | Notes |
|---|---|
| A weekly private class, 1 hour | Thursdays, 7 to 8 PM Central, on Google Meet. Teaching for 20 minutes, then two men take the "hot seat" and the group works their system with them. Max 10 men. |
| A monthly 1:1 with Brian, 45 minutes | Personal review of his Scorecard, his three systems and what's in the way. |
| Brian reviews his Scorecard every week | Men send a photo Sunday night; Brian replies by Tuesday with one line on what to change. |
| Direct line to Brian | Text or voice note, weekdays, reply within 24 hours. |
| Quarterly half-day intensive | Virtual, or in person in Huntsville: a 90-day plan for marriage, fatherhood and faith. |
| Every live group included | The 6-week group ($297 later) is free to Inner Circle men. |
| A couples session each quarter | One certified marriage counseling session for him and his wife, once a quarter ($100 value). |
| Everything in the Club | Portal, all tools on day one, monthly Club call, the community, plus a private Inner Circle space in it. |

**The difference in one line:** the Club gives a man the system and a partner; the Inner Circle gives him Brian, ten men who know his name, and someone checking his numbers every week.

## Brian's time (10 men)

- Weekly classes: about 4 to 5 hours a month
- 1:1s: 7.5 hours a month
- Scorecard replies and texts: about 6 to 8 hours a month
- Quarterly intensive: about 4 hours a quarter

Roughly 20 hours a month for $2,970 a month at full price. Two circles is the practical ceiling while he's also running the Club, couples and the free content.

## How it's sold

- Application plus a 15-minute call, not a "buy now" button. That's normal at this price and keeps the room right.
- **Founding circle:** first 8 men at $197 a month, locked in for as long as they stay, in exchange for honest feedback and permission to use real testimonials. Then $297.
- A clear promise: attend four Thursdays and send four Scorecards. If after 30 days it isn't worth it, full refund.
