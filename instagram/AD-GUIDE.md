**Headline:** `Free worksheet: Step 1`
**Website URL:** `https://go.bethemansystem.com/framework`
**Call to action button:** Download (or Learn more)

## Setup (Meta Ads Manager or the Instagram app)
Use **ads.facebook.com → Create** (the Instagram app's Promote button can also send people to a website, but Ads Manager gives you the controls below).

1. **Objective:** Traffic (or Leads if you want Meta to optimize for sign-ups). Destination: **Website**.
2. **Budget:** $2/day for 5 days = $10. Meta needs a few days to find the right people.
3. **Audience:** Men, 28-50, United States (or your country). Interests: Christianity, Fatherhood, Self-improvement, Personal development, Men's health. Aim for roughly 1M-5M people.
4. **Placements:** Manual. Instagram Feed, Stories and Reels, Facebook Feed and Stories. Deselect Audience Network and Messenger.
5. **Creative:** upload the feed image for Feed placements and the story image for Stories and Reels (customize by placement). Paste the copy above and the URL.
6. **Add a URL tag** `?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=step1` so you can see ad sign-ups separately.
7. Publish. Review usually clears within a few hours.

## What to expect
$10 usually brings roughly 10-30 link clicks, and perhaps a few worksheet sign-ups (cold traffic typically converts 10-30% on a free offer). Treat it as a test.

## After 5 days
Check: **Link clicks, Cost per click, and sign-ups on the framework page.** If cost per sign-up is under about $3, put in another $10-20 on the same ad. If clicks are cheap but nobody signs up, the page needs work. If clicks are expensive, change the audience before the image.

## Before you spend
- Click the ad link yourself on your phone and confirm the worksheet page loads fast and the sign-up works.
- Set the bio link and pin your best posts, since some people will check your page before signing up.
- Pay only through Meta's own billing. Anyone DMing you offering to "run ads cheaper" is a scam.
