## Setup (Meta Ads Manager or the Instagram app)
Easiest: open the Instagram app → your profile → **Promote** → pick a post (or upload the feed image as a post first, then boost it).
Better control: **ads.facebook.com → Create → Awareness or Traffic**.

1. **Objective:** Traffic → **Instagram profile** (cheapest for profile visits and follows). Awareness → Reach is the alternative if you only want eyeballs.
2. **Budget:** $5/day for 2 days, or $2/day for 5 days. $10 total. Pick the 5-day option if you can wait; it gives Meta more time to optimize.
3. **Audience:** Men, 28–50, United States (or your country). Interests: Christianity, Fatherhood, Self-improvement, Personal development, Men's health, Jocko Willink, Dave Ramsey. Keep it to roughly 1M-5M people. Turn **Advantage+ audience** off for tighter control, or leave on if the interests return a tiny audience.
4. **Placements:** Manual. Select Instagram Feed, Instagram Stories, Instagram Reels, Facebook Feed, Facebook Stories. Deselect Audience Network and Messenger.
5. **Creative:** upload the feed image for Feed placements and the story image for Stories/Reels (use "customize by placement").
6. **Destination:** your Instagram profile. If you use Traffic with a link instead, use `go.bethemansystem.com/framework` (free Step 1 page), never the $17 Club checkout.
7. Publish. Ads usually clear review within a few hours.

## What to expect
$10 usually buys roughly 1,500-4,000 people reached and a handful of profile visits. That is exposure, not growth. Treat it as a test.

## After 2-5 days
Check Ads Manager: **Reach, Profile visits, Cost per profile visit, New follows.** If cost per visit is under about $0.50, put in another $10-20 on the same ad. If not, change the audience before the image.

## Before you spend
- Pin your 3 best posts and set the bio link first so the people who land have something to see.
- Pay only through Meta's own billing. Anyone DMing you offering to "run ads cheaper" is a scam.
