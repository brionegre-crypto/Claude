## Step 2: Build a sustainable content strategy

### The 3 content pillars

#### Pillar 1: The CEO of You (run yourself like a business)
Treat your life like a company, with a mission, KPIs, quarterly reviews, and a "board meeting" with yourself.

- **Reel example:** *"I run my life like a Fortune 500 company. Here's my weekly board meeting."* Show a 30-second Sunday review: notebook, scorecard, three priorities. On-screen text: **"Weekly Review for Men: 15 min that changes everything"**
- **Carousel example:** *"Your Personal P&L: 7 Line Items Every Man Should Track"*
  Slides: Health → Wealth → Marriage → Kids → Skills → Faith/Mind → Brand. Each slide has one metric to track. Final slide: "Save this and do your first audit Sunday."

#### Pillar 2: Unbreakable Discipline
Routines, habits, identity, and doing it when you don't feel like it.

- **Reel example:** *"5 AM isn't the secret. This is."* Walk through the evening routine that makes the morning work (phone out of the bedroom, clothes laid out, top 3 tasks written down). Hook text: **"Why your morning routine keeps failing"**
- **Carousel example:** *"The Discipline Ladder: 10 Non-Negotiables, Ranked Easiest to Hardest"*
  From "make your bed" up to "hard conversation you've been avoiding." CTA: "Screenshot slide 2 and start at rung 1."

#### Pillar 3: Lead Your Home (fatherhood and family)
Being present, leading by example, marriage, and building legacy.

- **Reel example:** *"My son is watching how I handle stress."* A POV clip of you with your kids, voiceover on modeling behavior. Hook text: **"Your kids won't do what you say. They'll do what you do."**
- **Carousel example:** *"12 Questions to Ask Your Kids at Dinner (instead of 'how was school?')"*
  This is a highly saveable format. Final slide: "Send this to a dad who needs it."

### Posting cadence (sustainable for a busy dad)

| Day | Format | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Reel | Discipline |
| Tue | Carousel | CEO of You |
| Wed | Reel | Lead Your Home |
| Thu | Carousel | Discipline |
| Fri | Reel | CEO of You |
| Sat | Carousel | Lead Your Home |

Minimum viable version: 3 Reels and 2 carousels a week. Post Stories daily (3–5 frames, behind the scenes).

**Batching:** Film all Reels in one 2-hour block on Sunday, and design carousels in Canva from one reusable template (same fonts, same 2 brand colors).

### Content mix
- 40% educational (frameworks, how-tos)
- 30% relatable/story (personal struggles, dad moments)
- 20% motivational/identity ("The man you're becoming…")
- 10% promotional (lead magnet, offers)

## Step 4: Growth tactics

### Community engagement (30 minutes a day)
- **15 minutes before posting:** leave thoughtful comments (10+ words, not emojis) on 10–15 posts from bigger accounts in men's development, fatherhood, and fitness. Their audience sees you.
- **First 60 minutes after posting:** reply to every comment with a question to keep the thread going.
- **DMs:** Welcome new followers who engage. Use comment-to-DM keywords ("Comment STEP1") with ManyChat or Instagram's built-in automations to grow your email list.
- **Collabs:** Do one Collab post a month with a dad creator, fitness coach, or faith-based men's account. The post shows up to both audiences.
- **Story interactivity:** polls ("Did you hit your 5 AM today?") and question boxes ("What's your biggest discipline struggle?"). The answers become next week's content.

### "Saveable" content ideas for your audience

Saves and shares are the strongest ranking signals, so build posts that men will want to come back to.

1. **The Daily Man Scorecard:** a 10-point checklist (workout, read, no phone first hour, quality time with kids, etc.)
2. **Sunday Board Meeting Template:** 5 questions to review your week like a CEO
3. **The Dad Script:** exact phrases to use when your kid is melting down
4. **30-Day Discipline Challenge:** one task per day, shown as a calendar graphic
5. **Morning Routine Blueprints by time available:** 15, 30, and 60 minute versions
6. **Date Night Ideas Under $20:** for husbands leading their marriage
7. **Books Every Father Should Read:** a 10-book stack
8. **The Personal P&L:** 7 life metrics to track monthly
9. **Hard Conversations Playbook:** how to talk to your kids about failure, money, and respect
10. **Weekly Meal Prep Plan for Busy Dads:** discipline starts in the kitchen
11. **"If you only do 3 things today" checklist:** minimum effective day for a man
12. **Quarterly Life Review Worksheet:** a 90-day planning framework
13. **Screen-Time Rules for the Whole Family** (including Dad)
14. **The Anger Reset:** a 4-step protocol before you react to your kids
15. **Man's Annual Calendar:** yearly check-ins (health screening, will, anniversary, kids' 1:1 trips)

**Design rule for saveable carousels:** a bold promise on slide 1, one idea per slide, a summary or cheat-sheet slide at the end, and "Save this 💾" on the last slide.

## Step 5: Double down on what works (analytics)

### What to track weekly (in a simple spreadsheet or Notion)

| Metric | Where to find it | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Reach from non-followers | Insights → Reach | 50%+ on Reels |
| Saves per post | Post insights | Save rate of 2%+ of reach |
| Shares per post | Post insights | Your strongest virality signal |
| Profile visits → follows | Insights → Profile | 10%+ conversion |
| Link clicks / lead magnet opt-ins | Insights and your funnel | Grow week over week |
| Reel 3-second hold rate | Reel insights | 60%+ (tells you if the hook works) |
| Average watch time | Reel insights | More than 50% of Reel length |

### The weekly review loop (15 minutes, Sunday)
1. Sort the last 30 days of posts by **saves** and by **shares**.
2. Pick your top 3 posts. Note the pillar, format, hook, and topic.
3. **Remake the winners:** same topic, new hook; or a carousel turned into a Reel, and vice versa.
4. Cut or rework the bottom 3 topics.
5. Check which pillar drives the most **follows** and lean 10% more into it next month.

### 30-day checkpoint
- Which pillar gets the most saves? That's your authority pillar.
- Which gets the most shares? That's your growth pillar.
- Which drives the most link clicks? That's your conversion pillar.
Adjust the weekly schedule to match.
