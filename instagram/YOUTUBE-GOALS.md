## Why the thumbnail is built this way (CTR)

- **Three words, huge:** "WHY GOALS FAIL" still reads at the ~170px width of a phone feed thumbnail.
- **Curiosity gap:** it asks "why?" and the title promises the fix. They say different things on purpose, so they work together instead of repeating.
- **Face right, text left:** direct eye contact, a thinking pose that fits the question, large and well lit.
- **High contrast:** cream and amber on near-black, amber glow behind the head and a thin amber rim light so the cutout pops.
- **Branding kept tiny:** only the small "Be The Man" mark top-left. Bottom-right is left clear because YouTube puts the duration badge there.
