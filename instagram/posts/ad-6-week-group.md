## Setup notes

- **Audience:** United States, men 28–55. Interests: fatherhood, marriage, Christianity, Bible study. Advantage+ audience is fine to leave on.
- **Budget:** $10–15 a day for 7–10 days, starting this week. With four seats, stop the ad as soon as they're full; the checkout closes itself at 4 sales.
- **Policy:** Meta rejects ad copy that says or implies it knows a viewer's religion or family status (for example, "Christian husband, are you…?"). The copy above describes the group, not the viewer, so it stays inside that rule. Keep any edits the same way.
