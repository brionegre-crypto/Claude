Welcome to the club

You joined because you have meant it before. New Year's, a Sunday morning, the car after an argument. You meant it every time, and it did not hold.

This club exists to fix that. Not with more conviction, but with a system: a mechanism, not a mood.

Step 1: what type of man do you want to be?

↓Download the Step 1 worksheet (PDF)

Most men start with a goal. Step 1 comes before the goal. It asks one question, and the answer becomes the standard every later step is measured against.

How to work this step

  1. Name the attributes of the man you want to be.
  2. Make each one ordinary: what it looks like on a Tuesday.
  3. Apply the house test. Would they know without being told?
  4. Write the sentence that says who he is.
