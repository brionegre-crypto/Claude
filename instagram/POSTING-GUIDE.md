## Part 2: The posts, in order

**Ways to post:**
- **Right now, on your phone:** open Instagram, tap **+**, pick the file, paste the caption, and share.
- **Scheduled, from your computer:** go to business.facebook.com, then **Planner → Create post** (use **Create reel** for Reels). Tick Instagram, add the file, paste the caption, then choose **Schedule**.
- **Reels with music:** schedule them in the Instagram app instead. Add the audio, then go to **Advanced settings → Schedule**. Audio can't be added after a Reel is scheduled.
