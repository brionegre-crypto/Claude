## Why the thumbnail is built this way (CTR)

- **Three words, huge:** "WHY GOALS FAIL" still reads at the ~170px width of a phone feed thumbnail.
- **Curiosity gap:** it asks "why?" and the title promises the fix. The thumbnail and title say different things on purpose, so they work together instead of repeating.
- **Face right, text left:** direct eye contact with the camera, thinking pose that fits the question, large and well lit.
- **High contrast:** cream and amber on near-black, amber glow behind the head and a thin amber rim light so the cutout pops.
- **Branding kept small:** the amber "STEP 2" pill stays consistent across the series so viewers can find the next one. Bottom-right is left clear because YouTube puts the duration badge there.
