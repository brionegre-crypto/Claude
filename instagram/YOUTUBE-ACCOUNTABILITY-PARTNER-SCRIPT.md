## 0:40 STEP ONE: PICK THE RIGHT MAN (about 1:00)

Step one. Pick him. Four checks.

One, he shares your faith. He should be someone who'll bring Scripture into it, not just opinions.

Two, he'll tell you the truth. Not a yes-man. If you say, "I missed again," and he says, "That's okay, man," he's being nice. You need a man who asks, "What's the plan?"

Three, he'll answer a text. A great man who never replies is no use. Reliable beats impressive.

Four, he's working on something too. This goes both ways. If only you share, it turns into a confession booth, and it fades.

Where do you find him? Your church's men's group. A man in your small group. Someone already in your phone who's a step ahead of you. And don't wait to be assigned one. You have to ask.

## 1:40 STEP TWO: ASK HIM (about 55 seconds)

Step two. Ask him. And here's the secret: make it small.

Don't say, "Will you be my accountability partner?" That sounds like a lifetime. Say this:

"I'm trying to ___, and I keep dropping it. Would you trade a quick check-in with me once a week for eight weeks? It takes two minutes."

[ON SCREEN: "I'm trying to ___, and I keep dropping it. Would you trade a quick check-in with me once a week for eight weeks? It takes two minutes."]

Look at what's in there. One specific thing. A short time limit. And "trade," not "watch me." After eight weeks you can both decide to keep going. It's much easier to say yes to eight weeks than forever.

## 2:35 STEP THREE: THE SUNDAY ROUTINE (about 1:10)

Step three. This is what makes it last. Do it every Sunday night. Two minutes.

You each send one thing: your number. How many days you kept your plan, out of seven. Say "five out of seven." Not "pretty good." And the one miss that keeps coming back.

Then he replies with one line: "Got it. What's the plan for the misses?"

[ON SCREEN: "Got it. What's the plan for the misses?"]

That's it. He doesn't preach. He doesn't fix you. He doesn't shame you. He asks one question. And if he hasn't sent his by Monday morning, you ask him. That question is the whole job.

Do that for a month. Then compare. Did your number go up? Did the same miss show up less? If so, it's working. If not, bring it to him and change the plan.
