## The question, and why this one

**"Why can't I stay consistent?"**

What the research turned up (web search, so mostly blogs and ministry sites, not hard demand data; see the limits below):

- **Consistency is the problem men keep naming.** Christian sources repeat the same story: men start strong with prayer, Bible reading and habits, and can't sustain them. The common obstacles are no fixed time, no plan and no accountability.
- **Spiritual leadership is the other big one:** "how do I lead my wife and kids spiritually when I'm busy and don't know where to start?" The advice is the same shape: a weekly rhythm, a calendar slot and small steps. That's a good follow-up video.
- **Pornography is the heaviest pain point** (Barna's 2024 study with Pure Desire reports 75% of Christian men acknowledge viewing it to some extent), but it needs a different, careful approach and isn't this channel's lane right now. Don't build a growth video on it.
- **Systems over motivation is the winning idea in habit content,** and none of it is aimed at Christian men. That's the gap: the same advice (a fixed time, a cue, small steps, "never miss twice") for a man who prays, leads a home and fails by Wednesday.

**Why it fits your funnel:** it answers a question men ask in their own words, it's the next idea in your framework ("the laws of a system that holds"), and it leads naturally to your products. The Weekly Scorecard ($7, "your week on one page") is the exact tool for "a way to tell whether it held", and the 6-Week Group's December 6 session is this topic.

**Limits of the research, be honest with yourself:** I couldn't see YouTube search volume or your own analytics. Before filming, check two quick things: (1) type "why can't I stay consistent" and "christian men consistency" into YouTube's search box and see what autosuggests, and (2) look at your own YouTube Studio > Analytics > Research tab for what viewers search. If "how to lead your wife spiritually" shows more demand, swap it in.

- **Curiosity gap:** it names the failure and a day. The title promises the fix.
