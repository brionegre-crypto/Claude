# Script: "How to Pray With Your Wife (Even If It Feels Awkward)"

**Length:** 5 minutes (~700 words spoken at a calm 140 words a minute).
**Question it answers:** "How do I pray with my wife when it feels awkward?"
**Goal:** the viewer gets **The Prayer Strategy for Men ($9)**. Fallback: the free Step 1 worksheet.
**Tone:** calm, warm, a little humor. Talk to one man. No guilt.
**Shape:** hook → why it's awkward → three steps (ask, keep it short, same time) → what if it goes wrong → Scripture → the ask. One idea per minute.

`[YOUR STORY]` is the one slot I can't fill for you. I can't invent your marriage, so use a real moment, or cut the slot and the script still works.

Facts to keep exact (from your site): The Prayer Strategy for Men is $9, checkout `go.bethemansystem.com/227a8255`.

## 0:00 HOOK (about 30 seconds)

[ON SCREEN: "PRAY WITH YOUR WIFE (IT FEELS AWKWARD)" then cut to you, smiling]

Let me guess. You pray. You love God. And you've never really prayed out loud with your wife. Or you did once, a long time ago, it felt weird, and you never brought it up again.

If that's you, you're in good company. And here's the good news: it's not a spiritual problem. It's a starting problem. In five minutes I'll give you three steps you can do tonight.

## 0:30 WHY IT'S AWKWARD (about 40 seconds)

Why is it awkward? A few reasons, and none of them mean you're failing.

You don't want to sound fake. You don't know what to say. You're afraid of stumbling in front of the one person who knows you best. And maybe you've been a Christian for years and feel like you should have this figured out by now.

[YOUR STORY: one or two sentences about the first time you prayed with your wife, or the first time it felt awkward. A real, specific detail. If you don't have one: "The first time I tried, I said about eight words, and one of them was 'um.' She squeezed my hand anyway."]

Here's what I learned: she's not grading you. She's just glad you showed up.

## 1:10 STEP ONE: ASK, DON'T ANNOUNCE (about 50 seconds)

Step one. Don't announce it. Ask.

Don't say, "We're going to start praying together now." That's a speech, and it puts all the pressure on you.

Instead ask one question: "How can I pray for you this week?"

That's it. That's the whole opening. You're not leading a service. You're asking your wife what she needs and then listening. A lot of men find that question lands better than any prayer ever could. And it gives you something real to pray about, so you're not searching for words.

[ON SCREEN: "How can I pray for you this week?"]

## 2:00 STEP TWO: KEEP IT SHORT (about 50 seconds)

Step two. Keep it under a minute.

Hold her hand. Say what she told you. Thank God for her. Ask him to help with the one thing she mentioned. Say amen. That's thirty seconds.

You don't need big words. You don't need Scripture memorized. Plain words are fine. God isn't waiting for polish. He's listening for honesty.

And if you stumble, keep going. A prayer with a stumble in it is still a prayer.

## 2:50 STEP THREE: SAME TIME, SAME TRIGGER (about 40 seconds)

Step three. Pick a time and a trigger, so it doesn't depend on whether you feel like it.

Before bed, after the kids are down. After dinner. Whatever already happens every day. Tie it to that. "After the kids are down, we pray for one minute." It's the same idea I use for everything: a goal says what, a system says when.

Start with three nights a week. Not seven. Small enough that you can't fail.

## 3:30 WHAT IF IT GOES BADLY? (about 40 seconds)

Now the fears. What if she says no? What if it's weird? What if you miss a week?

If she's not ready, don't push. Tell her you'd like to pray for her, and then pray for her on your own. Be patient and be kind. You can't force this, and you shouldn't.

If it's awkward, that's normal. Awkward is what new things feel like. It gets easier.

If you miss a week, don't make it a failure. Just start again. The only way to fail at this is to quit.

## 4:10 WHAT SCRIPTURE SAYS (about 25 seconds)

First Peter 3:7 tells husbands to live with their wives "in an understanding way" so that your prayers are not hindered. How you treat her and how you pray are connected.

And Matthew 18:19 says that when two of you agree in prayer, God hears. Two. Not one man alone in the kitchen.

## Notes for filming

- **Timing:** this is tight. If you're running past five minutes, cut the "What if it goes badly" section to two lines. Don't cut the three steps or the ask.
- **One ask:** the Prayer Strategy ($9) is the only product ask. The free worksheet is the fallback. Don't add the group or the Club here. Five minutes can't carry a third pitch.
- **Say "first link"** out loud so viewers know where to click.
- **Don't promise a result.** "It gets easier" is honest. "Your marriage will change" is not.
- **Be careful with tone:** this video is for men, but it talks about your wife and other men's wives. Keep it kind and don't blame women or make this about who's in charge. The point is humility, not authority.
- **Verify before filming:** I paraphrased 1 Peter 3:7 and Matthew 18:19 rather than quoting them. Read the verses and use the translation you prefer.
