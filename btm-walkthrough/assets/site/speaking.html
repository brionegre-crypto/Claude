Speaking

Bring Brian to your men.

Brian Greene teaches Black Christian men to stop running their faith on good intentions and start running it on a system: a time, a trigger and a test for every decision that matters at home. Husband of eleven years, father of three, pastor of ten years, certified marriage counselor.

Tell Brian about your event

Talks men leave with something to do.

The framework

A goal says what. A system says when.

The seven steps of the Be The Man System in one session. Every man leaves with his one sentence written down and one decision with a time on it.

Marriage and home

The first ten minutes home.

Why the evening is decided at the front door, and the small mechanisms that change what a wife and kids see: the door, the bedtime questions, the conversation you keep postponing.

Humility

The low seat.

Five postures of humility from Scripture, measured by what a man does, not how he feels. Built around The Low Seat Check, with a second witness from his own table.

Formats.

Men's night or breakfast

One talk, 30 to 45 minutes, with questions after. Printable worksheets for every man if you want them.

Retreat

Two to four sessions across a weekend, built around the seven steps, with time for men to write and talk in small groups.

Conference session or breakout

A keynote or a breakout on any of the talks above, fitted to your theme and time slot.

Workshop, in person or online

A working session where men leave with their own plan on paper. Online sessions run on Google Meet.
