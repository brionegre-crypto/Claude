The 7-week challenge

The last week.

A system with you at the center will serve you. That's the problem. Built around yourself, even a good system becomes a more efficient way to get what you want. So the center isn't you. The man in your sentence from week one starts as the man God is making.

This week's action

Take everything you've written over seven weeks: the sentence, the three goals, the wall, the time, the miss, the number. Lay it out together. Then pray over it, out loud if you can, and ask one question: is this who God is making me, or who I want to look like?

Change anything that answers the wrong way.

The real test

Could someone in your house tell something changed in these seven weeks, without you telling them? Don't ask them. Just watch this week.

Text your brother one last time. Then decide whether you're done, or whether this was the start.
