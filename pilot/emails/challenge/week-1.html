Before any goal, decide who is doing it.

The 7-week challenge

Week one of the 7-week challenge starts today.

Most men start with goals. That's why the goals don't hold. A goal with no man behind it is a wish with a deadline. So this week you decide who is doing it.

This week's action

By Thursday at 9 PM, finish the Step 1 worksheet: What type of man do you want to be? Twenty minutes, a pen, one sitting.

You'll end with one sentence. Something like: "I am a man who is home when he's home, keeps his word to his wife, and prays with his children before they sleep." Yours will be different. Write it anyway, even if it feels too big.

How you'll know you did it

  • The sentence is written down, not just thought about
  • Someone in your house could see it in you without being told
  • You texted your brother this week: done, or missed

Next week, that sentence decides which of your goals stay.
