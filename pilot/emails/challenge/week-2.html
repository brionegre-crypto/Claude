Week two. Get last week's sentence in front of you.

Every man I talk to has ten things he's going to fix. Ten goals means none of them get a time. So this week you cut.

This week's action

Tonight, write down every goal you're carrying. All of them: the gym, the money, the temper, the phone, the prayer life, the side business. Then hold each one up against your sentence and ask: does this make me more like that man?

Keep three. Cross out the rest. Not forever, just for these seven weeks.

If a goal doesn't answer to your sentence, it's somebody else's goal: your father's, your pastor's, the man on your phone.

How you'll know you did it

  • You have exactly three goals written under your sentence
  • Each one connects to a word in that sentence
  • You texted your brother: done, or missed
