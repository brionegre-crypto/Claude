The 7-week challenge

Week six, and it's Thanksgiving week. Travel, family, late nights, a full house. This is exactly the kind of week your system has to survive.

Numbers as numbers, not stories. Four out of seven is four out of seven. Not "going well." Not "pretty good considering."

This week's action

On a piece of paper, draw seven boxes, one for each day from today to next Monday. Every night, mark your one action: a check if you did it at the time you set, an X if you missed. Fill it in that night, not from memory on Sunday.

When next week's email arrives, write the number at the bottom: something out of seven.

How you'll know you did it

  • Every box is filled in, checks and X's both
  • You have a number, not a description
  • You texted your brother: done, or missed, every night this week
