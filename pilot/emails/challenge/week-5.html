The 7-week challenge

Week five. By now you've probably missed at least once. Good. That's what this week is for.

Here's how most promises die: you miss one day, you feel like a failure, and by Friday you've quit the whole thing. The miss wasn't the problem. Not knowing what a miss means was.

This week's action

Under your day-and-time plan, write two lines:

  • A miss is: (for example, "the phone wasn't in the drawer by 7:45")
  • When I miss, I: (for example, "tell my wife that night and do it the next evening")

That's a failure condition. Decide it before the bad day comes, so the bad day doesn't get to decide for you.

How you'll know you did it

  • Your miss is something you could see, not a feeling
  • Your recovery happens within a day, not "next week"
  • You texted your brother: done, or missed
