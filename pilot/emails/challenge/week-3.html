The 7-week challenge

Week three.

You didn't fall short for lack of wanting it. Something stopped you, and it was probably the same thing every time. A plan that depended on how you felt. One bad day that turned into a bad month. An evening with no decision in it. A habit you inherited and never agreed to.

Mine was meaning it. I meant it every single time, and I changed nothing for years.

This week's action

Pick one of your three goals. Think back to the last time it fell apart. Write one sentence that finishes this: "It stopped when..."

Be plain about it. "It stopped when I got home tired and turned on the TV" is useful. "I lacked discipline" isn't. You can't build around a wall you won't look at.

How you'll know you did it

  • You wrote down a moment, not a character flaw
  • You'd recognise that moment if it happened again this week
  • You texted your brother: done, or missed

Next week, that moment gets a plan with a time on it.
