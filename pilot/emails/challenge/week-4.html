"Weekly" is not a time. Thursday at 7:40 is.

The 7-week challenge

Week four. This is the one that changes things.

Nothing exists until it has a time. "I'll be more present" is a goal. "Monday and Thursday, 7:40 PM, phone in the kitchen drawer until the kids are in bed" is a system.

Across many studies, people who wrote down when and where they'd act were measurably more likely to follow through than people who only decided to. In one, 91% of the planners did it, against 38% of everyone else.

This week's action

Take the goal from last week, the one with the wall in front of it. Write it as: day, time, place, action. Put it right where the wall was. If it stopped when you got home tired, the time is the moment you walk through the door.

Then do it at least once before next week's email.

How you'll know you did it

  • It has a day and a clock time, not "evenings" or "weekly"
  • You did it at least once this week
  • You texted your brother: done, or missed
