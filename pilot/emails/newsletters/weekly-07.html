“I’m thankful for my family” doesn’t count. Here’s what does.

THE THURSDAY EMAIL · THANKSGIVING

Say it at the table.

Happy Thanksgiving. This one is short, because today should be spent with them, not with your inbox.

Somewhere today, someone will go around the table and ask what everyone is thankful for. Most men will say “my family.” It’s true. It’s also the kind of thing nobody at the table will remember by Friday.

Gratitude that is specific gets remembered. So before dinner, take five minutes and write one sentence for your wife and one for each child. Not what they are. Something they did.

Here is the difference. “I’m thankful for your mom” versus “I’m thankful your mom sat up with your brother three nights in a row when he was sick, and never once complained.” One is a greeting card. The other is a moment a kid will repeat years from now.

Then say them out loud at the table. Say your wife’s first.

THE TIME

Five minutes before the meal. Write them down.

THE TRIGGER

Someone asks “what are you thankful for?” Or you ask it first.

THE TEST

Could each of them repeat back what you said about them tomorrow?
