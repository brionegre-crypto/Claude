THE THURSDAY EMAIL · STEP 3

Pattern, not excuse.

You’ve set goals like the ones on last week’s list before. Some of them more than once. Before you build anything new, look honestly at why the last attempt didn’t hold.

An excuse explains one bad week: work was crazy, the kids were sick. A pattern explains why it happens every time.

An example. A man tries three times to start praying in the morning. Every attempt dies the first week he stays up past midnight. The obstacle was never prayer. It was that his evenings had no end time. That is something a system can fix.

Four questions to push past your first answer:

  • When exactly did the last attempt stop, and what was happening that day?

  • Did the goal ever have a set time, or only good intentions?

  • Who knew about it, and did anyone notice when it stopped?

  • What did you tell yourself the first time you missed?

Be specific and be fair. Guilt is a mood, and moods don’t build anything. You’re making a list of what your system has to survive.

THE TIME

Fifteen minutes, one evening this week. Pick it now.

THE TRIGGER

The goal you’ve restarted the most times. Start with that one.

THE TEST

You’ve written down one obstacle you’ve never said out loud.
