A year-end review that ends with a plan, not a resolution.

THE THURSDAY EMAIL · MECHANISM 6

One evening before January.

Merry Christmas. Enjoy today and tomorrow. This one is for later in the week.

In a week, most men will make New Year’s resolutions. You already know how that goes: you’ll mean it, and by February it will be gone. A resolution is a feeling on a date. A plan has a when.

So instead, pick one evening between Christmas and New Year’s and give it an hour. Five questions, on paper:

  1. Read your sentence. Is it still true? Change it if it isn’t.

  2. Name three things that held this year, with evidence. Not “I was a better dad.” What actually happened, and when.

  3. Name the one pattern that cost you the most. The trigger that kept winning. Be specific.

  4. Pick one decision per pillar for January. Each with a time, a miss and a test. Three or four decisions, not twelve.

  5. Read it to your wife. Ask her what she’d add. She’s seen your year from the other side of the table.

THE TIME

One evening between Dec 26 and Dec 31. Pick it today.

THE TRIGGER

The house goes quiet. An hour, a pen, the five questions.

THE TEST

January 1st, you have a written plan. Not a resolution.
