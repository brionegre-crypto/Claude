December is coming for your system. Shrink it before it breaks.

THE THURSDAY EMAIL · MECHANISM 5

The smallest version that still counts.

The next two weeks will break more systems than any other part of the year. Travel, family in the house, kids off school, late nights, a different schedule every day. The plan that worked in October won’t survive it.

Most men respond by pausing everything until January. “I’ll start again after the holidays.” Then January comes, and they’re starting from zero, again.

Don’t pause the system. Shrink it. Build a holiday version that is so small it survives anything:

  1. Keep one decision per pillar. Cut everything else until January. One for faith, one for your marriage, one for your kids.

  2. Shrink each one until it fits a bad day. Not thirty minutes of prayer. Five. Not date night. Ten minutes on the porch after the house goes quiet.

  3. Attach it to something that will still happen. Your routine is gone, but coffee, meals and bedtime aren’t. Hook each decision to one of those.

  4. Keep the Sunday review. Even five minutes of it. That’s what makes January a continuation, not a restart.

THE TIME

This Sunday: write your holiday version before the week starts.

THE TRIGGER

Coffee, meals and bedtime. The things December can’t cancel.

THE TEST

On January 2nd you’re continuing, not starting over.
