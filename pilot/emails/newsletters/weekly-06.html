The review that decides whether the week holds, step by step.

THE THURSDAY EMAIL · MECHANISM 2

Fifteen minutes on Sunday.

Every system breaks eventually. What separates the men whose systems last from the men who start over every January is not that their weeks go well. It’s that they look at the week honestly before the next one starts.

That’s the Sunday review. Fifteen minutes. Here is exactly how to run it:

  1. Read your sentence. The one from Step 1. Out loud if you can. Everything else gets measured against it.

  2. Score the week from the page, not from memory. Which decisions held? Which didn’t? If you didn’t log it on the day, count it as unknown, not as a win.

  3. Name the trigger for every miss. A time that was never realistic? A situation you didn’t plan for? Or no decision at all?

  4. Change one thing. One. Move a time, shrink a decision, or add a backup plan for the trigger that got you.

  5. Pray over the week ahead. Specifically. The Tuesday you already know will be hard.

Missing once is not failure. A miss you never name is.

THE TIME

Sunday, same time every week. Put it in your calendar now as a repeating event.

THE TRIGGER

The calendar alert. Coffee, your scorecard, and a pen.

THE TEST

You finish with one written change for next week. Not a feeling. A change.
