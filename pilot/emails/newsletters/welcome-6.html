BE THE MAN · STEP 5

The empty pillar.

A man can hold one area of his life together for a while by letting the others slide. He gets serious at work and disappears at home. He fixes his health and stops praying. From the inside it feels like progress. From the kitchen table it looks like a trade.

Step 5 exists to stop that trade. The framework rests on four pillars. Each one carries weight, and the whole thing leans when one is ignored.

You can run the check this week, even before you have the worksheet:

  1. List the areas of your life where other people depend on you. For most men that is some version of their faith, their marriage, their kids, and their work.

  2. Take the goals you scheduled with the four rules from the last email and put each one under the area it serves.

  3. Circle the area with nothing under it.

Most men find the same thing. Their goals pile up under one or two areas, and one sits empty.

An empty pillar is not a failure. It’s information. It’s often exactly where the obstacle you found in Step 3 was hiding, because nothing in your plan was protecting it.

THE TIME

Your Sunday planning, ten extra minutes.

THE TRIGGER

Your scheduled goals, sorted by the area of life each one serves.

THE TEST

The empty area now has one small decision with a time on it.
