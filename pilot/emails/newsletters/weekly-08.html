“When things calm down” is not a date. Put it on the calendar.

THE THURSDAY EMAIL · MECHANISM 3

The conversation you keep postponing.

Every man has one. Money with your wife. An apology you owe. The talk with your son about what he’s looking at on his phone. You know exactly which one it is for you.

You’ve been waiting for the right moment. Here is the problem: the right moment is a mood, and moods don’t schedule themselves. The conversation keeps getting pushed to “when things calm down,” and things never calm down.

So treat it like anything else in your system. Give it a when.

  1. Name it in one sentence. Write down what the conversation is actually about. If you can’t say it in one sentence, you’re not ready to have it.

  2. Pick a time and ask for it. “Can we talk Saturday morning after breakfast? There’s something I’ve been putting off.” Asking ahead takes the ambush out of it.

  3. Write your first line. The hardest part is starting. Try: “I’ve been avoiding this, and that’s on me.”

  4. Listen more than you talk. Your job in the first ten minutes is to understand, not to win.

THE TIME

A specific day and hour within the next seven days.

THE TRIGGER

You ask for the time today. Out loud, not in your head.

THE TEST

By next Thursday, the conversation has happened. Not gone well. Happened.
