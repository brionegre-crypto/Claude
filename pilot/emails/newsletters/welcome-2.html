Twenty minutes, a pen, and the one question most men skip.

BE THE MAN · STEP 1

Did you write the sentence?

Most men who download Step 1 still have it sitting in a downloads folder a month later. Not because they didn’t mean it. Because “I’ll do it this weekend” is not a time.

So this week, one thing: finish Step 1. One question: what type of man do you want to be? Not in the abstract. On an ordinary Tuesday.

Work it in four moves:

  1. Name the attributes. Patient. Present. Faithful. Your words, not ones that sound good.

  2. Make each one ordinary. “Patient” becomes “I don’t raise my voice when homework runs late.”

  3. Apply the house test. How would the people you live with know, without being told?

  4. Write the sentence that says who he is.

Here is the kind of sentence you’re aiming for: “I am a man who is home when he is home, keeps his word to his wife, and lets his kids see him pray.” Every part can be checked on a given day. Every part is visible to someone else.

THE TIME

Tonight or Friday night. Twenty minutes, one sitting.

THE TRIGGER

The house goes down. Pen and paper, phone in another room.

THE TEST

Could someone in your house notice each part of your sentence without being told?
