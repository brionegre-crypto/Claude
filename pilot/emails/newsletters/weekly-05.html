The most important part of your evening happens at the door.

THE THURSDAY EMAIL · MECHANISM 1

The first ten minutes home.

The framework is finished. From here, every Thursday is one mechanism: a single decision with a time, a trigger and a test, built for an ordinary week.

We start at the door.

Most men come home still at work. The phone is in the hand, the last meeting is still in the head, and the first thing the family gets is a man who is physically there and mentally in the car. Nobody decided that. It just happens, every night, because nothing was built to stop it.

The first ten minutes set the tone for the whole evening. So give them a shape:

  1. Finish work in the driveway. Two minutes in the car. Send the last message, then put the phone face down.

  2. Phone in the drawer by the door. Not in your pocket. In the drawer.

  3. Find your wife first. One real question, not “how was your day.” Try “what was the hardest part of today?”

  4. Then each kid. Get down to their level for one minute each.

THE TIME

Every weeknight, the first ten minutes after you park.

THE TRIGGER

The engine turns off. That’s the cue, not your mood.

THE TEST

By Friday, would your wife say evenings felt different? Ask her.

Put it on your Weekly Scorecard as one of this week’s decisions, with the miss defined: phone out of the drawer before the kids are in bed.
