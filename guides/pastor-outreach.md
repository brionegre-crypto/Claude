# Selling the men's ministry kit to pastors

Kit checkout: https://go.bethemansystem.com/6aa4411c ($199, one licence per church or ministry)
Leader guide (buyers only): https://go.bethemansystem.com/school/course/ministry-kit

## Who to contact first

Five pastors or men's ministry leaders you already know. Not strangers, not a mass email. One sale from a pastor who knows you is worth more than a hundred cold emails, because he'll tell other pastors.

## Message 1 (text or email, personal)

> Pastor [NAME], I built something I think could help your men. It's a seven-week men's group kit: a leader guide with a timed plan for every session, worksheets, and a weekly scorecard. Each man leaves every night with one written commitment, a time to do it, and a partner who asks about it. Any man in your church could lead it.
>
> Could I send you the first session plan to look at? No pressure either way.
>
> Brian

## Message 2 (when he says yes)

Copy the session 1 plan from the leader guide into an email (or send him the Step 1 worksheet PDF), and add this:

> Here's how the first night runs. If it looks right for your men, the whole kit is $199 for your church, and you can print for every man in every group you run: https://go.bethemansystem.com/6aa4411c
>
> Happy to get on a 15-minute call and walk you through it: https://go.bethemansystem.com/085b95e4

## Message 3 (one week later, if no answer)

> Pastor [NAME], just following up on the men's group kit. If the timing's wrong, that's fine. If it would help, I'm glad to lead the first night with your men myself.

Leading the first night yourself is your strongest offer. Use it when it's practical.

## After a sale

- Ask for a short quote after their group finishes week seven, with permission to use his name and church. That's your first real testimonial.
- Ask which other pastor he'd recommend you talk to.
